# Busselton Volunteer Fire and Rescue station

## What it is

The Busselton Volunteer Fire & Rescue Service station (DFES), on the block between Albert St (north), Stanley Pl (west) and Cammilleri St (east), with Peel Tce to the south. OSM way 370721434. Some listings give its address as Harris Rd, which meets Cammilleri St at the roundabout just north-east.

It is not "The Fire Station" pub on Queen St, which is a different building (the old fire station).

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/fire_station/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.34795,-33.65177, half 40 m, so 0.078 m/px) with a labelled grid (`aerial_grid.jpg`). Single storey, so about ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** the building sits square to the town grid. The appliance end faces Albert St (bearing 346).
- **Main hall:** roof about 21 m across the front by 19 m deep with its eaves, terracotta tiles. One gable, its ridge running back from Albert St. Middle at about **115.347942, -33.651782**.
- **Apron:** a concrete apron about 14 m wide runs about 20 m from Albert St to the east two-thirds of the front. That is where the appliance doors must be.
- **Entry block:** a small flat-roofed block, about 4 by 3.6 m, stands out from the west half of the front. A small pad with a pit lid sits beside it.
- **West wing:** flat pale roof, about 5.6 m wide and 11.5 m long, along the west side. South of it is a concrete slab with a parked car, behind a brick screen wall.
- **South wing:** pale low-pitched roof with hips, about 11.5 m by 8.8 m, behind the hall. It is skewed slightly against the hall in the aerial.
- **Car park:** sealed, with marked bays, east of the hall, off a service road from Cammilleri St.
- **Trees:** Norfolk Island pines on the Albert St lawn and along Stanley Pl. A few palms by the car park.

## Walls and roof

From the 2019 Mapillary view of the west side (`mly_2019-06_58m_look121`) and the aerial. Hex values are approximate. There is no photo of the front.

- **Walls:** tan face brick `#C39F74`, plain. The wings are the same brick.
- **Screen wall:** a long brick wall about 1.8 m tall in front of the west wing.
- **Roof:** terracotta tiles, orange-red `#B9614A` in the aerial, pitch about 18 to 20°. Dark grey fascias `#4F5A5C`.
- **Wing roofs:** pale grey metal, nearly flat in the aerial. The 2019 photo shows a tiled skillion on the west wing, so it may have been reroofed since.
- **Appliance bays:** drawn as two roller doors, 4 m wide by 3.8 m tall, pale, in the gable end. Their number and colour are guesses.
- **Signage:** none seen. The spec has a plain board, "BUSSELTON VOLUNTEER FIRE & RESCUE", over the doors, red letters on white.

## What a driver sees

Coming along Albert St from Queen St, it is on the right past Stanley Pl:

1. Tall Norfolk Island pines on a lawn.
2. A wide concrete apron.
3. The tan brick gable end of the hall under its orange tile roof, with the roller doors.

From Stanley Pl you see the long tan brick west side, its low wing and the screen wall behind the pines.

## How the spec draws it

`routes/landmarks/fire_station.json`, generated by the scratchpad `lm3/gen_fire.py`.

- **Hall:** specHouse puts a gable's ridge along the block's longer side. To get the ridge running front to back, the hall is drawn 19.7 m wide by 19.8 m deep. In life it is about 20 wide by 17.5 deep at the walls, so it reaches about 1.5 m too far back. A `ridge: "x" | "y"` option on `SpecBlock` would fix this.
- **Wings:** the entry block and the two wings are low-hip blocks in pale metal.
- **Fittings:** the apron and slab are concrete paving. The screen wall is a brick fence.

## Unsure

- **Everything on the front:** how many appliance doors, their colour and size, where the personnel door is, and what the signs say.
- Whether the ridge really is in the middle. In the aerial it sits about 1.5 m east of centre, which may just be roof lean.
- **The roofs.** Whether the wing roofs are metal (2026 aerial) or tile (2019 photo).
- Wall heights and the roof pitch are estimated.
