# Bussell Highway landmarks (Cowaramup and Metricup)

The landmarks for the Bussell Hwy drive between Vasse and Margaret River, in `routes/landmarks/bussell_highway_looks.json`. `routes/test-cowaramup.json` is a short public test route through Cowaramup that lists the same file.

## Built

- **Cowaramup cow statues:** 19 cows and calves in 12 small herds along the highway through town, plus a pair on Candy Cow's verandah (21 in all). `cowaramup_cows.md`.
- **Candy Cow:** `candy_cow.md`.

## Looked at, not built

- **Margaret River Chocolate Company, Metricup:** 415 Harmans Mill Rd at the Harmans South Rd corner (OSM way 174749766, 115.0775, -33.7998), about 4 km west of Bussell Hwy. You can't see it from the highway.
- **Margaret River Dairy Company, Metricup:** on the east side of Bussell Hwy (OSM node 5127419621, 115.12159, -33.80696). The factory and shop is a big shed about 60 m back behind a car park, with a belt of tall gums along the highway in front of its southern half. The 2019 dashcam runs show only the white entrance wall with its signs and a glimpse of the building through the driveway. Not built. If it's wanted later, the entrance wall with plain lettering is what a driver sees.
- **Cowaramup main street:** I found no pub, hotel or hall facing the highway in OSM or the 2019 photos. The District Social Club is a low club building set back near Memorial Drive. The shops are rows of small weatherboard gables that the base builds well enough. The yellow weatherboard Moon Haven cottage at the Bottrill St corner is the most distinctive building after Candy Cow, and would be the next to polish. The golden "Free As A Cow" sculpture in Pioneer Park is a possible small layer later.
