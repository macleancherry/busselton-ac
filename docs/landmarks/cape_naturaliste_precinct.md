# Cape Naturaliste precinct: toilets, kiosk and tank

## What it is

What stands round the Lighthouse Tea Rooms on the car park side of the Cape Naturaliste lighthouse precinct. The tea rooms and gift shop (where you buy tour tickets) are in the eastern keepers' cottage, which is already in the cottages spec (`cape_naturaliste_cottages.json`). This spec adds the rest:

- the toilet block at the car park's west edge;
- the small gabled information and tour kiosk where the paths meet on the lawn north of the cottages;
- the round water tank in the garden bed at the tea rooms' north corner;
- the peppermints round them, put back after the clear.

Route: **cape-naturaliste.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_naturaliste_road_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerials `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_precinct/tea.jpg` (centre 115.01975,-33.53905, half 22 m) and `precinct.jpg` (centre 115.0195,-33.5390, half 45 m), and OSM.

- **Toilets:** 8.1 x 3.4 m at 115.019864, -33.539053 (OSM way 369992411 and its toilets node).
- **Kiosk:** 7.8 x 3.4 m at 115.019587, -33.538934, about 8 m north of OSM's information office node.
- **Tank:** about 3.9 m across at 115.019743, -33.539149, between the garden bed's two halves.
- Toilets and kiosk are 29 m apart on one line along the cottage row (bearing about 119°). The spec's centre is between them: **115.019726, -33.538994**.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **29°**, the same axes as the cottages spec.

## Walls and roof

- Toilets: cream render `#E2DCCD` (assumed), grey gable roof `#888B87`, three doors.
- Kiosk: cream sheet `#E9E4D4`, pale grey gable roof, door and windows to the lawn, an "INFORMATION" board, two picnic tables in front.
- Tank: pale concrete or render `#D4D1C9`, flat lid.
- Dark green trim `#3F5A4A`, as on the cottages.

## What a driver sees

Pulling into the car park: the toilet block among the peppermints on the right, the tea rooms cottage and its tank behind, and the kiosk across the lawn.

## Approximations

- The tank is square.
- The toilet block's walls are taller (3.2 m) because the build's ground rises about 0.5 m under it from the spec's middle.
- The garden bed is two low hedge strips.
- Not included: the accessible lookout at the tower's base (inside the lighthouse fence, part of the lighthouse layer's area), and the shaded picnic deck and playground on the west cottage's far side, which you can't see from the car park.

## Unsure

- The toilet block's wall material (render, limestone or Colorbond).
- What the kiosk is used for today (OSM calls it the information office) and its sign.
- Whether the tea rooms have tables outside on the car park side.
