# Fearn Avenue town square, Margaret River

## What it is

The town square on the west side of Bussell Hwy, opposite Willmott Ave. It was the old Fearn Ave, closed to cars and paved in the Main Street Makeover:
- The Shire of Augusta Margaret River ran it, Hassell designed it and BCP Contractors built it, 2020-22.
- It's the "Festival" precinct: markets, events and gatherings.
- Awards: PIA WA Great Place commendation 2021, AILA WA Civic Landscape Award of Excellence 2023.

OSM has it as a pedestrian way (1279902310), so the base shows a plain strip.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_square/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.0735,-33.95085, half 30 m, so 0.0586 m/px; overlay `ov.jpg`). About ±0.7 m.

- **Orientation:** opens **east onto Bussell Hwy (98°)**. It runs about 52 m back to a small roundabout at the car park, and is about 17 m wide between RiverSmith (north) and the Acton/Stocker Preston block (south).
- **Middle of the main blocks (the planters):** **115.073389, -33.950806**.
- **What's in it:**
  - pale paving
  - a row of six young trees along the north side and five along the south, plus one in the middle
  - a long flat-roofed shelter on posts near the highway end (about 3 x 9 m) and a smaller one on the south side (3 x 5.5 m)
  - timber benches and planter boxes
  - low zigzag timber seating fins
  - a planted rain garden at the west end

## Looks

From the aerial (no street photo since the rebuild). Hex values are approximate.

- **Paving:** pale limestone `#D3C5A8`.
- **Timber:** benches, planters and fins in brown timber `#8A6A48`, which matches Hassell's local timber and recycled materials.
- **Shelters:** white roofs `#ECEBE6` on posts.
- **Rain garden:** a low edged bed with native planting.

## What a driver sees

Driving south past Settlers and Target, on the right just before the hotel on the left:

1. A gap in the shopfronts with trees and a white shelter.
2. People at benches, and the paving running back to the car park.

## Clears

Nothing: no buildings stand in it, and its blocks are the low planters and the garden edge.

## Unsure

- The shelters' shape: they look flat-roofed from above, but they may be pergolas or sails.
- What the zigzag shadows are: seating fins or timber screens.
- Tree heights: they're young trees, guessed at about 7 m.
