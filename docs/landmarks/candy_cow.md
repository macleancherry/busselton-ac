# Candy Cow, Cowaramup

Spec: `routes/landmarks/candy_cow.json`, look in `routes/landmarks/bussell_highway_looks.json`.

## What it is

The fudge and sweets shop, Shop 3 on the corner of Bottrill St and Bussell Hwy. It's in a small timber shop complex on the east side of Bottrill St, just off the highway at the south end of the shops, with MuKau Giftware & Gallery (Shop 2, gallery upstairs) in its north end. OSM node 1826053355 (115.1027698, -33.8515104), on the shop's front.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial (Oct 2026) at 0.059 m/px, rotated 25° to the building's axes with a 2 m grid (scratch `cow/cc_rot.png`), and checked against OSM way 925796790. About ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **west-south-west onto Bottrill St** (bearing 245) across a brick-paved verandah and a row of angle parking. The north gable end faces the Bottrill St junction and Bussell Hwy, about 30 m away across the Wine Centre lawn.
- **Centre of the main blocks:** **115.102829, -33.851551**.
- **North end (MuKau):** 8.2 m along Bottrill St by 6.3 m deep, steep gable (about 40°), ridge along the street, gable end to the north.
- **Candy Cow:** 7.3 m by 6.1 m, low gable (about 22°) under the long grey roof.
- **South block:** 4.4 m by 5.8 m, hipped.
- **Rear wing:** 7.3 by 7.3 m behind the middle, with solar panels (outbuilding).
- **Verandah:** 1.6 m deep along Candy Cow and the south block, on posts; brick paving under it out to the parking.

## Walls and roof

From 2019 photos from Bussell Hwy (Mapillary). Hex values approximate.

- **MuKau:** brown weatherboard `#7B4A2E` above, white rendered shopfront `#EFEBE0` below on the north and front faces, under a grey skillion verandah on the north end. Its white render has brown cow-spot circles: not modelled.
- **Candy Cow and the south block:** cream walls `#E6D9B0`, white trim.
- **Roofs:** grey corrugated `#8E9396`.
- **Sign:** "CANDY COW" in plain cream capitals on a dark board on the verandah fascia. No logo; the cow mural on the wall isn't copied.
- **Statues:** a cow and a calf from the town's herd on the verandah (see cowaramup_cows.md).

## What a driver sees

North-bound, just past the BP and the park, on the right beyond the Wine Centre lawn: MuKau's brown gable with its white spotted shopfront, then Candy Cow's long low grey roof running back along Bottrill St, with the cow and calf out front.

## Unsure

- The MuKau shopfront is on its north gable end, but the spec's gable ends take the block's wall finish, so the gable reads brown weatherboard down to the white panel at 3 m, with no pediment detail.
- The verandah depth and the post spacing are guesses from the aerial.
- The front wall's mural and the shopfront details aren't modelled.
