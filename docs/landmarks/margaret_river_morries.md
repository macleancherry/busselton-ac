# The Tuckshop, ANZ and Morries, Arc of Iris, Margaret River

## What it is

The south end of the shops on the east side of Bussell Hwy, just before the Wallcliffe Rd roundabout. OSM maps three buildings as one 59 m footprint (way 1269006914):

- **The Tuckshop** (site 34): Nixon's electrical and bike shop, later the Tuck Shop, still a restaurant. A small gabled building with its name painted in an oval on the roof.
- **ANZ and Morries** (site 35, 149 Bussell Hwy): on the site of the 1935 Owen-Tucker house, later the bank manager's house. Now a flat-roofed block with the ANZ and Morries (breakfast to late-night cocktails, in every Margaret River guide).
- **Arc of Iris** (site 36, 151 Bussell Hwy): on the 1928-29 McLeod and Auger grocery and butcher site, now the restaurant under an olive-green hip roof.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerials `out/refs/mr_morries/aerial30.jpg` (centre 115.0738,-33.95335) and `aerial_n.jpg` (centre 115.0738,-33.9532), both half 30 m, so 0.0586 m/px; overlays `ov.jpg` and `ov_n.jpg`. About ±0.7 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **west onto Bussell Hwy (262°)**. The street bends slightly here.
- **Middle:** **115.073759, -33.953309**.
- **The Tuckshop:** a gabled shop about 10.6 x 10 m, its front about 4 m nearer the street than the ANZ. Flat-roofed back and side parts.
- **ANZ and Morries:** a flat-roofed block, 22.8 m along the street by 19.6 m deep.
- **Arc of Iris:** a hipped block about 17 x 23 m, set about 2.6 m back, with a lean-to verandah over the front.

## What a driver sees

Coming south to the end of the shops, on the left: the little gabled Tuckshop, then the tall dark gable of the ANZ and Morries front, then the green roof of Arc of Iris with Ruther Park opposite.

## Clears

It clears only OSM way 1269006914, its own footprint. The bank block to the north (way 1269006935) stays.

## Unsure

- The 2019 photos are seven years old: the ANZ branch, Morries' front and the colours may have changed.
- The Tuckshop's roof lettering isn't drawn (specs can't paint roofs). It has a sign board instead.
- The Arc of Iris front and the lean-to are partly hidden by a street tree.
