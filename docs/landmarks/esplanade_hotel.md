# Esplanade Hotel

## What it is

Two-storey pub and hotel at 30-38 Marine Terrace, Busselton. Designed by H. Cowell Ashton, built 1899 and opened July 1900. Added to in 1908, refurbished in 1941, owned by the Gray family since 1952. Function rooms added on the west side in 2019. Listed on the City of Busselton heritage list (Federation Free Classical, stuccoed brick and iron).

Note: it is **not** on the Queen St corner. It's mid-block on the south side of Marine Tce, between Queen St (about 170 m west) and the Stanley St / Jetty Way junction (about 60 m east). The OSM pub point (115.34542,-33.64695) sits in the hotel's car park, about 28 m south-east of the building's middle.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/esplanade_hotel/aerial_front.jpg` (centre 115.34516,-33.64680, half 45 m, so 0.088 m/px), using a labelled pixel grid. Lengths are along and across the building's own axes. One storey of lean in the photo, so allow about ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces NNW (bearing about 346°) onto Marine Tce. The street and the building both run at about 76°/256°.
- **Middle of the two-storey main block:** **115.345231, -33.646700**.
- **Two-storey main block:** about 42.5 m along the street by 17 m deep. Rectangular. Main hip roof with its ridge parallel to the street, plus short hip and gable wings off the back.
- **Single-storey front infill:** about 30.5 m wide and 7.5 m deep, built across the front of the main block. Its roof is a row of about six small street-facing gables (the "serrated roofline" in the heritage record). From above the gables look like clear or white ribbed sheeting.
- **West wing (2019 function rooms):** about 18 m wide by 25.5 m deep, flush with the front line on the west side. Low-pitch roof behind a white parapet edge. Single storey.
- **East wing:** narrow (about 7 m) single-storey wing with hip roofs, running back along the car park side.
- **Whole complex:** about 62 m along Marine Tce and about 37 m deep, not counting the long accommodation block that runs south from the back of the west wing.
- **Setback:** the front of the infill sits about 11.5 m from the Marine Tce traffic kerb. In between, from the road: a planted island, a parking lane (cars park nose-to-kerb), the footpath and the front fence. The front fence is roughly at the back of the footpath, about 4 m in front of the infill. The two-storey wall is about 7.5 m behind the infill front, so about 19 m from the traffic kerb.

## Walls and roof

From the street photo `wah_0.jpg` (date unknown, probably mid-2010s) and the aerial. Hex values are eyeballed from photos and aerial and are approximate.

- **Walls:** smooth render painted off-white `#EDEAE2`. Three pedimented bays are painted mid grey `#8A8D91` with white trim: one at the east end, two side by side at the west end. The façade is not symmetrical.
- **Height:** two storeys. Main parapet about 8.5 m. The grey bays' triangular pediments peak at about 10 m. Small curved (segmental) pediment in the middle of the white centre section, about 9.5 m.
- **Roof:** corrugated iron in muted red, `#8E3B40` (reads `#9D6A71` in the sunlit aerial). Main hip about 25-30°. Mostly hidden behind the parapets from the street.
- **Chimneys:** the record mentions stucco-decorated chimneys. A small box at the west end of the parapet roof is visible in the photo. Not confirmed from the aerial.
- **Upper-floor windows (street face, left to right):**
  - east grey bay: 2 tall round-headed sash windows, about 0.9 x 2 m
  - white centre section: about 4-6 plain rectangular sash windows, about 0.9 x 1.4 m, partly hidden by street trees
  - two west grey bays: 2 round-headed windows each
  - Window frames are white, with dark glass.
- **Ground floor:** almost all hidden behind the gabled infill. The infill is open-fronted or glazed, with white posts. Each small gable has a dark red-brown barge board `#6E2B26`, a white lattice gable infill and a white timber lattice frieze below. Gable apexes are about 4.5 m. Entrance doors are glazed, behind the fence.
- **Signage:** "ESPLANADE" and "HOTEL" in dark red-brown serif capitals on the white parapet, about 0.7 m tall, one word either side of the central curved pediment. Draw as plain lettering only.
- **East wing (from the street):** single storey, flat or low roof, white walls, red-brown fascia over a verandah. A kids' play area with a slide sits in front of it, behind the fence.

## Grounds

- **Front fence along the footpath:** square rendered piers about 1.5 m tall, spaced about 4 m, with capped tops and black metal palisade panels on a low wall. In `wah_0` the piers and wall are grey `#9A9893` with pale caps. In the newer close-up `wah_2` they are cream `#E6DFC6` with white palisade. **Use cream if building it new**, but this isn't confirmed.
- **Front terrace (beer garden):** between the fence and the infill. Paved in buff `#C9B79A`. Black bar tables and grey chairs, young street trees in round grates, large pots. Several big square umbrellas: yellow and blue "WA Ale" ones, grey ones seen in the aerial at the east end of the terrace.
- **West corner:** a large dark grey shade umbrella or sail, about 7 m across, in front of the west wing.
- **Kerbside:** a planted island with low pink-flowering shrubs, then the parking lane. A green timber power pole stands in the island right in front of the centre of the hotel.
- **Car park:** big open asphalt car park to the east and behind, with a shade sail over a back courtyard. Entered from Marine Tce and Stanley St.
- **Bottle shop:** a small separate red-roofed cross-shaped building at the north-east corner of the car park, beside Marine Tce (the Thirsty Camel POI).
- **Other buildings on the site:** a long red-roofed accommodation block runs south from the back of the west wing, and a newer red-roofed block sits at the south-east of the car park.

## What a driver sees

1. A long white two-storey façade with grey pedimented bays: one at the left end, a pair at the right.
2. "ESPLANADE" and "HOTEL" in big dark lettering along the parapet.
3. A row of about six small red-trimmed gables with white lattice across the ground floor.
4. Rendered fence piers with black or white palisade, and umbrellas over the beer garden behind.
5. A planted island and parking lane between Marine Tce and the hotel, with a power pole in front.

## Unsure

- The current fence colour (grey vs cream) and the umbrella branding. The two photos differ and neither is dated.
- The exact count of upper-floor windows in the white centre section, because trees hide some.
- Whether the "small rotunda in the north-east corner" from the heritage record still exists. In the aerial that corner shows only umbrellas, and there is a small white arched structure near the middle of the terrace.
- The roofing of the infill gables. From above it looks like clear or white sheeting, not iron.
- The pitch of the main roof, which is estimated, not measured.
