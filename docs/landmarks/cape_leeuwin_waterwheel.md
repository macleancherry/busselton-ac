# Cape Leeuwin Waterwheel

## What it is

An overshot timber waterwheel built in 1895 by the lighthouse contractors to pump spring water to the building site and then the keepers' cottages, through a hydraulic ram. Calcium-rich spring water has crusted it solid with lime, so it no longer turns and looks like stone. On the State Register since 2002. It sits just above the high-tide line in a small cove about 1 km north of the lighthouse (about 400 m north of the cottages), next to a small car park on Leeuwin Road. The lighthouse is in view to the south.

Route: **cape-leeuwin.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_leeuwin_waterwheel/wheel.jpg` (centre 115.135501,-34.368772, half 25 m) and the inHerit description.

- **Wheel:** at the west end of the straight timber flume, where the ground drops to the cove: **115.135377, -34.368805** (OSM's attraction point is 12 m east, at the viewing area).
- **Flume:** a timber trough of three planks with cross-ties, about 60 m long, running east (bearing about 85°) into the scrub, then a fibreglass trough. It rests on the ground except near the wheel, where limestone piers carry it.
- **Walls:** the wheel's shaft rests between two limestone walls.
- **Hydraulic ram:** remains of its air chamber on the wheel's south side.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **175°** (south, to the car park and viewing area), so the wheel is seen side-on.

## Walls and roof

- Wheel and walls: lime-crusted, grey-white `#D2CBBB` and `#C9C1AE`.
- Flume: weathered timber `#7A6A55`.
- A timber viewing deck and rail in front (assumed), with a small sign.

## What a driver sees

It's below the road on the sea side at the small car park, 145 m off the route line. Mostly a stop-and-look landmark, not one seen on the move.

## Approximations

- **The wheel** is a disc of three crossed boxes (a stepped twelve-sided outline), about 4 m across, with no paddles.
- **The flume** is level on four piers (14 m of it), not following the ground.

**Wants a Blender layer:** a round wheel with its rim and paddles under the lime crust, the flume on its piers down the slope.
