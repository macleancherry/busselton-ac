# Collie Motorplex race tower

## What it is

The race control tower ("race tower" on the club's facility list), on the paddock side of the main straight, level with the start line. Spec: `routes/landmarks/collie_race_tower.json`. The same spec includes the small timing or officials' hut in front of it by the wall.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial (centre 116.24345,-33.43025, half 45 m, 0.088 m/px), rotated 38° so the straight runs up the image (`colliem/tower_rot.jpg`). About ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** the long axis is parallel to the straight (bearing 38°) and the front **faces the track (bearing 308°)**.
- **Middle:** **116.243457, -33.430311**.
- **Size:** about 12.8 m by 4.4 m. A timber stair and landing on the back (east) side add about 2 m.
- **Height:** its morning shadow is about 6-7 m long, so it is two storeys, about 6.5 m.
- **Setback:** front wall about 24 m from the centreline of the straight (18 m from the track edge). The start line is level with its north end.
- **Timing hut:** about 4.5 x 4.4 m, single storey, 18 m further down the straight and 5 m nearer the track (12 m from the edge).

## Walls and roof

- **Upper floor:** white walls `#ECEBE4` with a band of glazing along the track side. A 2024 photo from the far end of the paddock shows a white upper storey with a dark window band.
- **Ground floor:** modelled in face brick `#76503F` to match the other paddock buildings. Not seen in any photo.
- **Roof:** flat, white, with a deep overhang (modelled 1.2 m on the track side).
- **Stair:** brown timber with rails, from the south up to a landing near the north end of the back wall.
- **Signage:** "RACE CONTROL" in plain white lettering on navy between the floors. This is invented; nothing was readable in the photos.

## Grounds

- Concrete apron round the base.
- To the north: the clubrooms (khaki roof) with two round white shade canopies between them.
- To the south and east: the spectator lawn with shade sails (`collie_spectator_lawn`).
- Between it and the track: dirt runoff, then a concrete wall or fence line along the paddock edge.

## What a driver sees

On the run down the main straight it is the one tall building on the left, right at the start line: a white box with dark glazing over a brick base. The pedestrian bridge is about 45 m further on.

## Unsure

- The ground floor's finish and whether it is open underneath (stilts) or enclosed.
- Whether the glazing faces the track only or wraps the ends.
- What the hut by the wall is.
