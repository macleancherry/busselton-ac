# The Good Olive building and Dome, Margaret River

## What it is

The two-storey pair on the east side of Bussell Hwy, opposite the Visitor Centre, where the shops start. Main Street Historic Walk sites 12 and 13:
- **Site 12:** the lot stood vacant until a modern development. Now a Thai restaurant and The Good Olive (food and gifts).
- **Site 13:** a 1978 laundromat, surf and food complex, rebuilt in 2003 as the Dome cafe building.

Neither is heritage listed. OSM maps both, plus the shops south of them, as one 30 x 72 m footprint (way 1269006931, tagged "Dôme").

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_goodolive/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.0746,-33.9488, half 30 m, so 0.0586 m/px; OSM overlay `ov.jpg`; the south half is in `aerial_s.jpg` and `ov_s.jpg`). About ±0.7 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **west onto Bussell Hwy (277°)**. The north side faces the side street opposite Tunbridge St.
- **Middle of the main blocks:** **115.074492, -33.948817**.
- **Good Olive building:** an L of pale grey hip roofs:
  - a west wing about 14 x 8.5 m behind a 2.7 m deep two-storey verandah on the street
  - an east range about 20.5 x 16 m
  - a single-storey annex on the north-west corner
- **Dome:** a flat-roofed two-storey block, 17.5 m along the street by 25 m deep, front about 4.5 m back from the verandah line.
- **The rest of the OSM footprint:**
  - a flat-roofed shop block (19 x 24 m) and a barrel-roofed block (11.6 x 14 m) further south
  - in the spec as plain single-storey outbuildings, because the spec clears the whole OSM footprint and these would otherwise leave a gap

## Walls and roof

From 2019 street photos (Mapillary). Hex values are approximate.

- **Good Olive:**
  - white render `#EBE7DD` and a pale grey Colorbond hip roof `#D4D6D2`
  - a two-storey verandah on slim white posts: the balcony has a white balustrade, with glazed rooms behind it upstairs
  - a dark brown shopfront `#4A3C33` with "The Good Olive" board
- **Dome:** sand-coloured render `#CFB48C`, tall upper windows (arched in reality), a small black juliet balcony, a parapet, and a dark awning `#3F4446` over the footpath.

## What a driver sees

Coming south past the Visitor Centre on the right, it's the first big building on the left:

1. The white two-storey verandah on the corner.
2. The sand-rendered Dome next to it, with its tall upstairs windows.

## Clears

It clears only OSM way 1269006931, its own footprint (checked against every OSM building in the town). The neighbours north across the side street and south (way 1269006938) stay.

## Unsure

- The 2019 photos are seven years old. Tenants (Thai restaurant, Dome) may have changed.
- The balcony wraps round the north side too. The spec only does the street side.
- How the L roof divides between the two-storey and single-storey parts behind.
