# The Banksia Tavern

## What it is

A small bar at 43 Prince Street, just off Queen Street. It opened in July 2023 in the old Bodega bar's space; the site has also been a craft beer bar, a café and a shop. Inside it's long and narrow, with about 80 seats: velvet booths, a timber bar, stone walls and vinyl records. Still open (reviewed in 2026).

## Walls and roof

Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:** painted parapet front, off-white `#E4E0D6`. Red brick `#A8553F` on the laneway side.
- **Height:** single storey, about 4.6 m to the parapet.
- **Awning:** cantilevered (no posts), about 2.8 m deep at about 3.3 m. Teal-green fascia `#3E6B67`, metal soffit.
- **Shopfront:**
  - grey tiled piers `#9EA2A2` at each side and a tiled stall riser
  - a grey aluminium frame with a big window and a row of highlight glazing above
  - a glass door at the east end
- **Signage:** a timber venetian blind `#5B3E2B` behind the window, lettered **THE BANKSIA TAVERN** in gold `#D8AA3A`. Plain lettering here; the small logo left out.

## Grounds

- **Footpath:** grey and white patterned concrete pavers under the awning.
- **Laneway:** the brick-paved laneway to the west, with the red-brick side of the next building beyond it.
- **Neighbours:** a row of narrow shops with awnings either side. Busselton's banks and Dymocks are around the corner on Queen Street.

## What a driver sees

A small one-shop front in a row, easy to miss from Prince Street:

1. A teal awning edge.
2. A dark window with a brown venetian lettered in gold.
3. The laneway and red-brick wall just to its left.
