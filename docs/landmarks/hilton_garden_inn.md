# Hilton Garden Inn Busselton

## What it is

A 110-room hotel on the Busselton foreshore, south of Foreshore Parade and east of Jetty Way. It was built for Collab Capital, designed by Collard Preston (lead architect Nick Preston), and opened on 29 December 2023. Not heritage listed.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial (`out/refs/hilton_garden_inn/aerial35.jpg`, 0.068 m/px, and `aerial60.jpg`, 0.117 m/px) and checked against the OSM building outline (OpenFreeMap tiles). The roof outlines are read at roof level. The building leans a little in the aerial, so the roof may sit 1 to 3 m off the true base.

- **Shape:** an L made of two wings, turned about 25° from north-south. The long axis runs on a bearing of 155°/335°.
  - **Gable wing (west, the tall part):** roof about 16.5 m wide (between the eaves on the north gable end) and 33 to 37 m long, running NNW to SSE. The south end is cut at an angle: the west edge is about 33 m and the east edge about 37 m. OSM's ground outline for this wing is 21 x 35 m, probably because it includes the balconies and the ground-floor podium.
  - **East wing (flat roof):** about 17.5 m deep (N-S) and runs about 30 m east from the gable wing. Its far east end is at 115.34646,-33.64552 (approx.). The roof is flat, with a white parapet and a large block of rooftop plant in the middle.
  - **Overall:** about 51 m (WSW-ENE) by 37 m (NNW-SSE). OSM gives 1,560 m² on the ground.
- **Faces:** the glazed gable end faces NNW (bearing about 335°) onto **Foreshore Parade**, looking at the jetty and the bay. The hotel entrance and porte-cochère are on the **west** side, off a driveway loop from Jetty Way. The pool garden sits in the angle north of the east wing.
- **Setback:** the gable end is about 5 to 7 m from the south kerb of Foreshore Parade, with the angle-parking kerb, footpath and a narrow garden and terrace fence in between. The east wing is about 15 to 18 m back, behind the pool lawn.
- **Roof:** the gable wing has one big gable roof, ridge along the long axis (NNW-SSE), with gable ends north and south. Pitch is roughly 30°. The east wing is flat behind a parapet. A low, separate flat roof (a stair or link) sits between the two wings on their north side.
- **Centre (lng,lat):**
  - Middle of the gable wing's roof: **115.345991,-33.645590**.
  - Middle of the whole L (OSM ground outline): 115.346138,-33.645585.

## Walls and roof

- **Storeys:** 5 (ground plus 4) in both wings. The top floor of the gable wing is the Geographe function room, which opens to the cathedral ceiling under the gable. Rough heights (from storey counts and photo proportions):
  - ground floor about 4.5 m
  - gable wing eaves about 17.5 m, ridge about 22 m
  - east wing parapet about 17 to 18 m
  - OSM has `render_height=19`.
- **Walls:**
  - **Main cladding:** dark charcoal vertical-rib metal cladding on the gable wing's side walls and the gable infill above. About `#2a2d31`.
  - **Gable front:** a thick white rake fascia and soffit with downlights, about `#ecebe6`. The soffit and balcony walls show warm and pale (cream or light timber-look, about `#d9c7a5`) in the night photos, but daytime colour is unconfirmed.
  - **Podium:** the ground floor is mostly glass between white or light-grey posts and beams, about `#e6e6e2`.
- **Roof colour:** charcoal or dark grey, about `#3a3f45`. The aerial reads bluish, `#415465`, from haze. The east wing's flat roof is mid grey (`#9aa3a8`) with a white parapet cap (`#e9ebe6`).
- **Gable end (north face, from Foreshore Parade, left to right = east to west):**
  - **Ground floor:** a glass restaurant front (Together & Co) behind a dark slatted fence, with two large cream umbrellas on the terrace.
  - **Floors 1 to 3:** three balcony bays per floor with black metal balustrades. The left bays are wider. The rightmost (west) bay is a smaller balcony box that sticks out past the corner.
  - **Floor 4:** a full-width terrace with a frameless glass balustrade.
  - **Gable triangle:** almost entirely glazed in two rows of tall panes (the function room). Dark mullions sit under the white rake fascia.
  - On the right, a narrow dark-clad strip with a column of windows wraps onto the west side.
- **West side (driveway side):**
  - Dark ribbed cladding, with about 5 columns of narrow vertical windows over 4 floors (floors 1 to 4). Each window has a thin white or light-grey frame and a small projecting horizontal sunshade.
  - One larger white-framed window spans about two floors, near the north corner.
- **East wing:** balconies on every floor (black balustrades, cream or white walls). The first-floor balconies have frosted-glass panels. The thin white roof fascia oversails the balconies.
- **Porte-cochère:** a long flat white canopy on slim white posts. It runs along the west side from the podium corner south past the end of the wing, about 5 to 6 m deep and about 25 to 30 m long. Fascia about 0.6 m deep, underside about 4 m up. Lit downlights and linear lights are underneath.
- **Signage:** the hotel name as white plain lettering, about 0.6 m tall and 7 m long, sitting on top of the north end of the porte-cochère fascia and facing west to the driveway. Upstream draws the text only: "Hilton Garden Inn" (the red logo square sits to its left in reality; leave it out).
- **No chimneys.** The plant on the east wing roof is behind the parapet and hardly visible from the street.

## Grounds

- **North side (Foreshore Parade):**
  - a dark slatted terrace fence (about 1.2 m) with a low clipped hedge and black bollards in front
  - the restaurant terrace with umbrellas
  - a gravel and native garden bed with a red-ochre mulch patch at the west corner
- **Pool:** a rectangular pool (about 12 x 7 m) in a lawn garden north of the east wing, with sun loungers. It is fenced, with low shrubs and a few palms and trees.
- **West side:** a driveway loop from Jetty Way under the porte-cochère, with a pale paved footpath and native garden beds along Jetty Way.
- **South:** a large open asphalt car park (about 40 spaces) between the hotel and Stanley Street, with a small grey service building at its south-west. Peppermint-type trees are in the islands.
- **East:** a native scrub and peppermint strip, then the big public car park.

## What a driver sees

1. A tall glazed **gable end** facing the bay, with a thick white outline, on top of three floors of balconies. It is the tallest thing on the foreshore.
2. Dark charcoal **vertical ribbed cladding** on the long side walls, with small sunshades over narrow windows.
3. A long, flat white **porte-cochère** with the plain white hotel name on top, on the Jetty Way side.
4. A lower flat-roofed **east wing** with balconies, behind the pool lawn.
5. The terrace fence, umbrellas and hedge right on the Foreshore Parade footpath.

## Unsure

- **Daytime colours:** both usable photos are at dusk. Wall and soffit colours are estimates. The roof colour comes from the aerial.
- **East wing height:** whether the east wing roof is exactly level with the gable wing's eaves.
- **Gable wing outline:** the exact ground outline. The roof (16.5 m wide) and OSM (21 m wide) differ. The south end of the gable wing may be cut at an angle or may just look that way in the aerial.
- **Number of window columns** on the west side beyond those visible in the photo, and the treatment of the south and east faces (no photos found).
- **Sign size:** a guess from the photo.
