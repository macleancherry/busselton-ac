# The Goose

## What it is

Beachfront restaurant and bar on the Busselton foreshore, just south-east of the base of the jetty. Designed by South West Architects and built in 2005, with forms based on the old Jetty Interpretive Centre. Badly damaged by fire in 2021 and reopened in 2023. Not heritage listed.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/the_goose/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.34544,-33.64463, half 40 m, so 0.078 m/px) with a labelled pixel grid. Single storey, so lean is small. About ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** the long axis runs ENE-WSW (about 76°), parallel to the beach. The **north side faces the beach** (bearing about 346°). The south side faces the foreshore lawn and the shared path.
- **Middle:** **115.345421, -33.644646**.
- **Size:** about 39.5 m long by 20 m deep at the east end and 23.5 m at the west end. A rough rectangle with a stepped south side.
- **Roof outline:**
  - **Three large gables** with ridges running north-south (towards the beach), each about 9.5 m wide, side by side across the middle and east of the building. Gable ends face north and south. Pitch about 40-45° (steep, A-frame look).
  - A **row of four small gables** along the north (beach) side, each about 5 m wide and about 8 m deep. These are verandah roofs on posts over the outdoor dining, with their gable ends facing the beach.
  - A **flat or low-pitch roof** over the south-west corner (kitchen and service), with air-con units on it, about 12 x 14 m.
  - Lower flat or skillion roofs link the gables. Dark strips of solar panels lie on the slopes of the middle and east gables.
  - An **open deck** with umbrellas at the north-east corner, about 13 x 5 m.
- **Setback:** it sits in the foreshore park, not on a street. About 75 m west of the Busselton Jetty car park and about 100 m north of Foreshore Parade. The beach promenade wall is about 10 m north of the building's north side.

## Walls and roof

Mostly from the architect's photos (2005 building) and a 2015 photo. See "Unsure" about the rebuild. Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:**
  - gable ends: horizontal weatherboard-style cladding (the architect lists Primeline "Newport" infill boards) painted charcoal-navy `#2B3441`, with broad white barge boards `#F2F2F0`
  - lower walls: rendered brick, white `#EDEDE8`
  - long sides: mostly floor-to-ceiling white-framed glazing and bifold doors
- **Height:** single storey. Walls about 3.2 m to eaves. Big gable apexes about 8-9 m. Small verandah gables about 5.5 m.
- **Roof:** Colorbond in a white or off-white, `#E6E8E6` (reads `#9CAAAB` on the shaded side in the aerial).
- **Gable windows:** each big gable end has a small triangular window high in the apex with white framing. Below it is the charcoal cladding, then a full-width glazed wall with a white frame grid of about 1 m panels.
- **Verandahs:** the four small gables on the north side stand on slim white posts about 3 m tall, about 4.5-5 m apart. The gable faces are white or cream, fully open below. A white pipe balustrade about 1 m tall runs around the deck edge.
- **Signage:** "the goose" in white lowercase lettering on the charcoal cladding of a big gable end, about 0.4 m tall, centred under the apex window. There is a white goose figure above the words, but **draw the name as plain lettering only, no goose logo.**
- **Chimneys and parapets:** none seen.

## Grounds

- **Decks:** timber decks on the north and east sides `#B98B66`, with outdoor tables and black or wicker chairs.
- **Umbrellas:** large square umbrellas, white and navy or white and pink, on the north-east deck.
- **Hedge:** a low rosemary hedge (green) along the deck edges on the lawn side.
- **Grounds:**
  - mown lawn on the south and south-east sides
  - pale concrete promenade and paving on the north and west
  - several tall palms along the east side and around the jetty car park
- **Shared path:** the dark grey shared path (old rail alignment) curves past the south-west corner. The jetty and the Jetty Interpretive Centre are about 80 m to the north-west.
- **Neighbours:** the Busselton Visitor Centre (solar-panelled roof) is about 40 m south-east. Shelter Brewing (large dark sawtooth-roofed building) is about 100 m south-west.

## What a driver sees

There is no street frontage. From the jetty car park or Foreshore Parade, a driver sees it across lawn and palms:

1. Three steep white roofs with charcoal gable ends, standing out against the beach.
2. White barge boards and triangular apex windows.
3. A cluster of small white gables (the verandah) on the beach side, seen side-on.
4. Palms and umbrellas around it.

## Unsure

- **The rebuild after the 2021 fire.** I found no clear post-2023 exterior photo. The current Landgate aerial shows the same layout as the 2005 design (three big gables plus four small verandah gables), now with solar panels. So it looks rebuilt or repaired to the same form, but cladding colours and signage may have changed. Worth a street-level check.
- Which big gable carries "the goose" lettering today. In 2005 it was on a gable end facing the lawn or deck.
- Roof colour: white in the photos, but it may be a pale grey Colorbond such as Surfmist or Shale Grey.
- The OSM building outline is larger (about 41 x 32 m bounding box) because it is axis-aligned. Use the measured 39.5 x 20-23.5 m.
