# Augusta Hotel motel wing

## What it is

The Augusta Hotel's two-storey motel wing, south of the hotel on Blackwood Avenue, on your left as you pull away from the start of the Cape Leeuwin route. Its long side faces the road behind a small car park.

Route: **cape-leeuwin.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the same aerials and frame as `augusta_hotel.md`.

- **Block:** 29.5 x 10 m, 25.5 to 55 m after the start, 16 m back from the road's centre line. The upper floor is carried 1.5 m over a ground-floor walkway on nine square posts.
- **Car park** in front.
- Middle **115.160615, -34.317171** (the spec's centre).
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **248°**.

## Walls and roof

- Cream render `#EFE7D3`.
- Red terracotta tile hip roof `#B0583A`. It is the only tiled roof in the complex; the aerial shows its texture.
- Room doors and windows along the ground floor, and windows along the upper floor.

## What a driver sees

A long cream two-storey block with a tiled roof and Norfolk pines in front, on the left in the first 50 m of the drive.

## Approximations

- The build's ground falls about 0.8 m towards the south end, so a plinth runs under the block.
- The motel's rear wings (running back from its south end, and along the back of the complex) are left out. The ground behind falls up to 4 m on the fallback heights.
