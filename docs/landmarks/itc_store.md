# International Trading Company Store (former), now REDSEA Gallery

## What it is

83 Bussell Hwy, at the north entrance to the main street shops, on the east side. Built in 1925 next to the old hall: the third store in the new town, after Peake's and Lamb's. Over the years it held groceries and hardware, drapery, an Elder Smith agency and the Floral tearooms. From 1974 it was Moondyne Art and Crafts, then the Margaret River Art Gallery. Jahroc took it on in 2002 and added a contemporary gallery in 2005. REDSEA Gallery has been there since 2024, after a sympathetic renovation that kept the original timber. On the Shire's heritage list (MR(T)-04).

## Footprint

**Not measured, and not placed yet.** The inHerit record and a 2019 street photo give the form. I couldn't match the lot on the aerial with confidence: Overpass was down, so I had no OSM building outlines or addresses. The spec is a typical size for the description.

- **Orientation:** front faces **west onto Bussell Hwy** (bearing about 275°).
- **Shop:** about 8 m along the street by 14 m deep, single storey, gabled to the street.
- **2005 gallery:** a flat-roofed block, about 12.5 m long, beside the shop to the north (in the photo). The record describes the 2005 work as a link between the shop and the rear cottage. The spec has both.
- **Rear cottage:** gabled, behind (outbuilding).
- **Provisional "at":** the building at 115.07459, -33.94686 (the first commercial building south of the side street at the north end of the shops, from the aerial `mr/itc2.jpg`). It's not in `margaret_river_looks.json` until this is checked.

## Walls and roof

From the inHerit record and the 2019 photo (Mapillary). Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:** square-edged timber weatherboards with an oiled finish, which reads dark brown `#4F3D2E`. Faint old painted signs on the north wall ("Teas", "ACC").
- **Roof:** Zincalume and corrugated iron, `#B8BCBD`.
- **Front:** a central splayed entry with timber double doors, big shop windows in simple timber frames either side, and a modern bull-nosed verandah over the footpath.
- **2005 gallery:** black walls, deep red-pink fascia `#A8324A`, high strip windows under a deep fascia.
- **Signage:** "REDSEA Gallery" in plain lettering. No logo.

## What a driver sees

Coming into town from the river end, the first old building on the left: a dark timber shop with a pale gable and a black modern gallery beside it, behind a garden with sculptures.
