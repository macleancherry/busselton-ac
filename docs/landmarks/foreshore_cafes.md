# Foreshore cafes: Kyst and Sandbox

## What it is

**Kyst** (restaurant, plus an outdoor "Jetty Bar") and **Sandbox** (takeaway and cafe facing the playground) share one building on the Busselton foreshore. It sits between the beach and Foreshore Parade, about 150 m south-west of the base of the jetty, with the foreshore playground to its east. The address is 1 Foreshore Parade.

This is the old **Equinox** cafe. Peter Gordon ran it from 2000. It was rebuilt and enlarged in 2015, with the entrance moved to the west and the takeaway expanded. The business and its leasehold were offered for sale in 2022 (about 620 m² cafe/restaurant, 233 m² bar and 241 m² covered takeaway), and it has traded as Kyst and Sandbox since then. The building is publicly owned (City of Busselton land) and leased. Not heritage listed.

**Still trading (Oct 2026):**
- **Kyst:** yes. OpenTable lists it open daily 8:30 am to 9 pm, with reviews into October 2026.
- **Sandbox:** listed on the Margaret River Region site and in OSM, "beside the playground and across from the jetty". I found no dated 2026 review, so it isn't confirmed.

The two names are one building, so this is one spec.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerials `out/refs/foreshore_cafes/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.34345,-33.64512, half 45 m) and `aerial_close.jpg` (centre 115.343439,-33.645221, half 30 m, 0.059 m/px, 5 m grid in `aerial_close_grid.jpg`). Single storey, so lean is small. The south and east edges are good to about ±0.5 m. The north edge is under fig canopy, so it's about ±2 m.

- **Shape:** an irregular spread of wings, about 47 m across (WNW-ESE) by 25 m deep at the west end and 38 m at the east end. The north side runs at about 100°, so the building gets deeper towards the jetty.
- **Spec frame:** x along bearing 115°, y along 25° (towards the beach). The front faces **205°** (SSW, towards Foreshore Parade and the Queen Street end).
- **Middle of the blocks:** **115.343434, -33.645187**.
- **Roof outline:**
  - **West wing (Kyst dining):**
    - hipped roofs with solar panels, about 18 x 10 m
    - a second hipped room behind it, under the trees
    - two **gables pushing south** (each about 6 m wide), their gable ends to the road, with flues between them
  - **Middle:** a big low-pitched hipped roof over the dining room and kitchen, about 14 x 30 m, covered in solar panels.
  - **East wing:** the tallest roof, a gable about 14 m wide with its ridge running NNE to the beach (about 25 m long). It carries solar panels on its east slope. The east wall runs straight along the playground path, and the Sandbox servery is at its north end.
  - **South-east tip:** two more gables pushing south (6 m and 4.5 m wide), their ends facing the road. These are the building's southern-most point.
- **OSM nodes:** Kyst at 115.343297,-33.645138 (west part) and Sandbox at 115.343582,-33.645096 (north-east part), both inside this building.

## Walls and roof

From the OpenTable photo (`ot_hero.jpg`, a gable end with the KYST sign) and the aerial. Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:** horizontal weatherboard, very pale grey `#CDD0CC`.
- **Gable ends:**
  - broad white barge boards and fascia `#F3F3F0`
  - bronze-framed glass `#3B302A` running up into the apex in a triangle
  - a weatherboard band at about 3 m
  - floor-to-head glazing in bronze frames below
- **Roof:** white or Surfmist Colorbond `#E8E9E5`, with many solar arrays (dark strips) on the west, middle and east roofs. Pitches:
  - about 30° on the front gables
  - 25° on the east wing and west hall
  - about 14° on the middle
- **Height:** walls about 3.4 m to the eaves. The east-wing apex is about 7 m. The front gables reach about 5.2 m.
- **Signage:**
  - "KYST" in white capitals on a round black sign on a gable's weatherboard band. **Draw plain lettering only**, not the knot logo above it.
  - "SANDBOX" at the takeaway on the east side, near the playground.

## Grounds

- **Paths:** pale concrete on the south and east, with a curved path along the playground side and the shared path to the west.
- **South-west nook:** a small timber deck and courtyard with a pergola.
- **Jetty Bar beer garden:** on the beach side, under big old figs and Norfolk pines, looking across the lawn to the bay and the jetty. It is shown here as tables and white umbrellas behind a low fence.
- **Trees:** very large figs north of the building (pale-barked, as in the photo), a big canopy tree off the south front, and pines along the beach.
- **Neighbours:**
  - the foreshore playground (pirate ship and water play) directly east
  - a white tensile shade structure at the south-east corner
  - Foreshore Parade loops past to the south
  - the beach promenade is about 30 m north

## What a driver sees

From Foreshore Parade, across a strip of lawn and under a big tree:

1. A long, low, pale grey building with a white roof.
2. Four white-barged gable ends facing the road, with dark glass up into their apexes. The KYST sign is on one.
3. The tall east gable behind, with the playground and umbrellas to its right.

## Unsure

- **Which gable carries the KYST sign.** The photo's view (the jetty reflected, lawn and sea to the right) suggests a gable end on the east or north-east side. The spec can only put lettering on south-facing walls, so it goes on the west front gable.
- **Sandbox:** its trading status in 2026, and exactly where its servery and sign are. OSM puts it at the north-east end.
- **The north half.** The roof shapes under the fig canopy are guessed, simplified to one hipped room and the long east gable.
- **The beer garden.** Its position and furniture are assumed.
- **The 2015 renovation** clad steel posts in recycled jetty timber. That isn't seen in the photo and isn't modelled.
- **Surroundings:** the jetty is about 150 m to the north-east, so the foreshore playground and the tensile shade are other agents' or the base's.
