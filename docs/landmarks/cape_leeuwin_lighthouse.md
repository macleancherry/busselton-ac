# Cape Leeuwin Lighthouse

## What it is

The lighthouse on Cape Leeuwin, where the Indian and Southern Oceans meet, south of Augusta. Opened in 1895-96 by Premier John Forrest and automated in 1982. At 39 m it is mainland Australia's tallest lighthouse. The light is 57 m above sea level, and the climb to the gallery has 176 steps. Built of local limestone quarried within half a mile; foundations go 7 m down to granite and the walls are about 2.1 m thick at the bottom.

Route: **cape-leeuwin.json** (Augusta to Cape Leeuwin). Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_leeuwin_lighthouse/leeu.jpg` (centre 115.1360,-34.3753, half 120 m) and `out/refs/cape_leeuwin_lighthouse/llh.jpg` (centre on the tower, half 15 m).

- **Tower:** round, about 7.8 m across at the foot, on a square base. Middle of the base at **115.136325, -34.375062**. In the aerial the tall tower leans about 13 m north, so the dome sits over 115.13633,-34.37499, where OSM places it.
- **Gallery:** at the sixth floor (about 33 m), on the masonry outside the lantern.
- **Lantern:** white Chance lantern, 3.7 m (12 ft) across, curved glazing, domed cap and vent; total height 39 m.
- **Setting:** a bare, rocky granite point. The sealed lighthouse road ends in a paved forecourt round the tower; boardwalks lead out to lookouts on the east and west.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **0°** (north, back up the road to the cottages and car park).

## Walls and roof

- Tower: limestone, painted white `#F1F0EA`; small windows up the north and south faces lighting the seven floors.
- Square base: white.
- Gallery and rail: white.
- Lantern: white frame, dark glazing `#26313A`, white dome.

## What a driver sees

From the end of the route the tower stands 39 m tall over the rocks, 575 m south, white against the sea, with the three cottages in line in front of it.

## Approximations

- **Round, tapering tower:** three tiers of crossed bars and squares (stepped twelve-sided), each narrower. The same for the gallery (three overlapping slabs), the lantern pedestal, the glazing and the dome (a steep hip).
- **Square base:** a 9.2 m square plinth 1.4 m high (height assumed).
- **Gallery rail:** solid white panels round the stepped outline.

**Wants a Blender layer:** the true round taper, the corbelled gallery and rail, the curved glazing, the dome and vent.

## Unsure

- **The base.** "Cylindrical tower on a square base" (Wikipedia); the aerial shows a square-ended white shape at the foot. Its height and whether it is a plinth or a small porch building are guesses.
- Diameter and taper (from the aerial, ±0.5 m).
- The base position (from the aerial's lean, ±3 m).
