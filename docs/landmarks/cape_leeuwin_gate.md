# Cape Leeuwin precinct gate

## What it is

The gate where Leeuwin Road leaves the visitor car park and enters the lighthouse precinct: coursed tan limestone piers with caps and short limestone walls either side of the road, in the line of the precinct fence that runs along the car park's south edge. Just inside, east of the road, stand a round corrugated water tank and a thick hedge of coastal shrubs. The route drives through it on the way to the lighthouse.

Route: **cape-leeuwin.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_leeuwin_gate/gate.jpg` (centre 115.1362,-34.3720, half 45 m), Mapillary (2019 dashcam run through the car park) and the current build's own meshes.

- **Gate:** on the road at **115.135951, -34.371933** (the spec's centre; OSM's gate node is 115.135953, -34.371933).
- **Fence line:** bearing about 89.5° (OSM way 219412327). The base already builds it, as a Colorbond fence, with a 16 m gap at the road (local x -7.5 to 8.2 m from the gate). The spec's walls fill exactly that gap.
- **Walls and piers:** pier, 3 m wall and pier each side; the piers' inner faces 3.15 m west and 4.0 m east of the gate point (the built road is 5.5 m wide here, centred 0.7 m east of it, and the west Armco runs 2.85 m west).
- **Tank:** about 4.6 m across, 4 to 8.5 m east of the road just behind the wall.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **359.5°** (north, to the car park and the arriving driver).

## Walls and roof

From the Mapillary photos:

- Limestone piers and walls, coursed, tan `#CFB07A`, with paler caps `#E2D3B0`. Piers about 1.9 m, walls about 1.5 m.
- Tank: zincalume corrugated `#C4C8C4`, ribbed conical lid.
- The fence along the car park is grey weathered timber pickets, about 1.5 m (the base's Colorbond stands in for it).

## What a driver sees

Coming down through the car park towards the lighthouse, two stone gateposts and walls with the name on the left, the tank and shrubs just inside, then the open precinct lawns and the cottages.

## Approximations

- The opening is 7.2 m between piers; the real one is about 4.5 m. The piers stand clear of the built road and the Armco so the drive through is fair.
- The build's ground falls about 0.5 m to the east across the gate (the 30 m fallback heights), so the east piers and wall are shorter blocks over plinths that go down into the ground, keeping each about its real height above its own ground. Those heights are for the current build's ground.
- The tank is square under an eight-sided lid.
- The hedge is shrub trees (a hedge box can't follow the slope).
- The sign on the east wall ("CAPE LEEUWIN LIGHTHOUSE", plain lettering) is assumed.
- No gate leaves: the real gate is a pale steel gate, open in visiting hours.
