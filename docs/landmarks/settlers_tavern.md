# Settlers Tavern

## What it is

The main street pub, 114 Bussell Hwy, on the west side between the Visitor Centre and the Margaret River Hotel. Built in the 1970s as the surf crowd's local, still a pub, bottle shop and live music venue. Next to the 1980s Settlers Arcade. Not heritage listed.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_settlers/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.07387,-33.94989, the OSM node, half 50 m, so 0.0977 m/px; scratchpad copy `mr/tavern.jpg`). **Medium confidence:** from above it's one block of joined grey roofs with the arcade to its south, so the split between them is a guess.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **east onto Bussell Hwy** (bearing about 96°). A big public car park is behind it to the north-west.
- **Middle:** **115.073693, -33.949923**.
- **Hall:** about 27 m along the street by 31 m deep, under grey hip roofs, set back about 12 m.
- **Front bar:** a lower hipped range about 18.6 m long in front of the hall.
- **Beer garden:** a white-roofed covered area, about 18 x 8 m, between the front bar and the footpath, with a white umbrella on the footpath.
- **Street gable:** a gable bay about 10.7 m wide at the south end of the frontage, coming forward to the footpath.

## Walls and roof

From 2019 street photos (Mapillary). Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:** cream weatherboard `#E6DCC3`.
- **Gables:** pale timber barges `#CFB98C` on cream gable ends.
- **Verandah:** tall pale timber posts carrying an upper balcony with a timber balustrade.
- **Roof:** Colorbond, mid grey `#8F969A`, low pitch.
- **Signage:** "Settlers Tavern" in plain lettering. No logo.

## Grounds

- Red brick footpath, street trees, a timber deck under the covered beer garden.

## What a driver sees

Driving south through the shops, on the right just past the middle of the strip:

1. Grey roofs and the cream timber gable on tall posts.
2. The beer garden and umbrellas along the footpath.

## Unsure

- Where the tavern ends and the Settlers Arcade starts. The gabled, balconied frontage in the photos may belong to the arcade, not the pub. Worth a street-level check before polishing.
- Whether the white-roofed street-front area is the beer garden or a separate shop.
- Wall colours: the photos are from 2019.
