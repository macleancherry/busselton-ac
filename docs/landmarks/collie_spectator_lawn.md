# Collie Motorplex spectator lawn

## What it is

The green lawn between the race tower and the paddock amenities, beside the main straight just before the pedestrian bridge. It's the nearest thing Collie has to a grandstand: there is no grandstand or built spectator mound. Spec: `routes/landmarks/collie_spectator_lawn.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial, rotated 38° (`colliem/tower_rot.jpg`). About ±0.8 m.

- **Middle (the fire pit):** **116.243369, -33.430533**, facing the track (308°).
- **Shade sails:** a pinwheel of four triangular sails, alternating cream and navy, about 14 m across, centred about 15 m north-east of the fire pit.
- **Barbecue square:** an L of pale concrete about 13 x 12 m with a round fire pit and picnic tables.
- **Trees:** three jacarandas (purple in the aerial) along the paddock road.
- **Setback:** the sails start about 24 m from the track edge, behind the paddock fence.

## Walls and roof

No building. The sails are on steel posts, about 2.5-3.6 m at the corners. The fire pit is modelled as a low brick ring.

## What a driver sees

On the left of the main straight, just before the bridge: a patch of green lawn with cream and navy sails, behind the fence.

## Unsure

- The sail layout is read from above. The corner heights are guesses.
- The real fire pit may be a steel bowl rather than brick.
