# Saltwater (Busselton Performing Arts and Convention Centre)

## What it is

Saltwater, the City of Busselton's performing arts and convention centre at 9 Queen St. It opened on 9 May 2026 and was designed by Kerry Hill Architects. It stands on the east side of Queen St between the Marine Tce roundabout and the Weld Theatre, opposite the Old Courthouse.

- **Inside:** three levels. A 640-seat tiered theatre (it converts to a 1,000-person flat floor), the A-Class gallery on the ground floor, and a level 2 conference suite with an alfresco balcony looking over the bay.
- **Next door:** two heritage buildings belong to the project:
  - the 1931 Agricultural Bank on the corner (ArtGeo), which becomes the centre's bar and restaurant (`artgeo.md`)
  - the Weld Theatre to the south, kept and joined on behind (`weld_theatre.md`)

**Not in OSM.** The accepted base (`out/accepted/busselton.zip`, 31 Oct) has **nothing on the block**: no Saltwater, no ArtGeo and only a small box behind ArtGeo. ArtGeo is in OSM (way 737080959) but is missing from the base. The likely reason is the Old Courthouse landmark's clear circle across the road.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial (`out/refs/saltwater/salt_aer.jpg`, centre 115.3440,-33.6470, half 70 m, 0.137 m/px), rectified square to the building (`salt_rect.jpg`, `weld_rect.jpg`). The building sits square to Queen St, whose street front faces bearing 257.

- **Overall:** about 55.5 m along Queen St by 40.7 m deep, all flat roofs.
- **Street band:** a low band about 7.5 m deep runs the whole frontage. It holds the gallery and foyer.
- **Conference block:** the tall north-west mass, 14.5 by 31 m, with a narrow lower band (3.5 m) along its north side. That band is where the level 2 balcony looks north to the bay.
- **Plant deck:** the middle back, with plant visible on its roof. A lower block sits in front of it.
- **Auditorium and fly tower:** the largest roof, 20.5 by 31 m, in the south half.
- **Service band:** about 4 m wide along the south side, plus a 2.2 m strip along the back on the car park lane.

## Walls and roof

There is **no usable photo of the finished building**. The image search turned up nothing, the venue site blocks scripted access, and the newest Mapillary view is from 2021, before construction. Everything below is a guess in a restrained Kerry Hill manner:

- **Walls:** pale warm render or precast `#DDD6C8` and `#D4CCBC`. The plant deck and service band are warm grey metal `#A59F94`.
- **Street front:** floor-to-ceiling glazing behind a 3.2 m cantilevered canopy, with glass doors in the middle.
- **Conference block:** two glazed bands on its street face and along its north side.
- **Roofs:** white membrane or Colorbond in the aerial.
- **Signs:** "SALTWATER" in plain dark letters, high on the auditorium's street face and small on the canopy edge. No logo.

## What a driver sees

Both the Foreshore and Sights layouts go round the Queen St / Marine Tce roundabout:

1. ArtGeo's terracotta roof on the corner.
2. Behind it, the pale stepped mass of Saltwater.
3. Its long glazed front down Queen St, opposite the courthouse cafe.

## How the spec draws it

`routes/landmarks/saltwater.json`, generated by `gen_saltwater.py`. A copy of the script is in `out/refs/saltwater/`, with its helper `lmkit.py`. It draws in the rectified-aerial frame and shifts so that the front glazing is y=0.

- **Masses:** nine blocks, each a flat-ish hip (pitch 3°). The heights are guesses: street band 5.6 m, balcony band 9.2, middle 9.8, plant deck 12, conference 13.2, auditorium 17, service and back 6.5 to 8.
- **Balcony:** a roof slab at 12.9 m over the north band, with a dark balustrade panel. specHouse has no raised floors, so the balcony is the top of the 9.2 m band.
- **Glazing:** dark glass panels with window parts over them.
- **Footpath:** no paving. The base has the footpath, and paving would widen the landmark's clear circle.
- **Clear circle:** about 38 m. Plotted against OSM (`chk_salt.jpg`), it takes in no generated building except the ones these specs replace.

## Unsure

- **The finishes:** all the facade materials and colours, the glazing pattern, the canopy, and where the entry is.
- **Heights:** every height, especially the fly tower. Shadows in the aerial only show that the auditorium is the tallest part.
- **Signage:** whether "SALTWATER" lettering is on the building at all.
