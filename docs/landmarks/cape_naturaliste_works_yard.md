# Cape Naturaliste works yard

## What it is

The light station's works yard, north-east of the keepers' cottages and reached off the car park's northern loop: the 1940s store and shed and the 1948 garage from the inHerit record, all timber-framed and clad in asbestos-cement sheet, round a sealed yard. Back of house, but you see it across the trees from the car park loop. It replaces the plain boxes the base builds from the OSM outlines.

Route: **cape-naturaliste.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_naturaliste_road_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_precinct/works.jpg` (centre 115.01960,-33.53875, half 25 m) and OSM ways 297320447, 297320452 and 297320456.

- **Gabled shed:** 6 x 9.1 m, gable end to the north-north-east (OSM 297320447).
- **Store:** 5.5 m square, near-flat roof with two vents (OSM 297320456).
- **Garage:** 6.5 x 13.6 m, hipped (OSM 297320452).
- All three are on the cottage row's axes; the yard sits between them. A curved stone wall closes the yard's north side.
- Middle **115.019556, -33.538746** (the spec's centre).
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **29°**, the same axes as the cottages spec.

## Walls and roof

- Cement sheet, cream `#E4E0D3` (the store greyer, `#D9D6CB`).
- Pale metal roofs `#DCDEDA` (they read white on the aerial).
- Yard: grey seal `#77756F`.

## What a driver sees

Pale sheds through the peppermints on the right, past the cottages, as you go round the top of the car park.
