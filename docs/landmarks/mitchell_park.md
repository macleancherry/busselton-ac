# Mitchell Park

## What it is

Mitchell Park is the town-centre park between Queen Street (east), Prince Street (south) and the Busselton Central shopping centre (west), with the old fire station on its north-east. The City rebuilt it in 2021-22 with:

- a big oval lawn ringed by hexagon paving
- seating walls, raised decks, Corten bench boxes and festoon lighting
- new paths and lights

The lawn and paving are ground. The structures that stand up are two **tensile fabric shade canopies** at the ends of the lawn and a cluster of **shade sails over the playground** in the north-west corner.

## Footprint

- **Middle of the spec** (halfway between the canopies): **115.344206, -33.650800**.
- **North canopy:** at about 115.344237,-33.650643, on the lawn's north edge.
  - About 7.5 m across, six fabric panels from a central top down to posts round the edge.
  - Red, with one white panel facing south onto the lawn.
  - On a paved pad with seats.
- **South canopy:** at about 115.344230,-33.650958, on the lawn's south edge.
  - The same size.
  - Dark green, with a white panel facing north.
  - On a paved pad with low planter walls either side.
- **Playground sails:** about 115.344152,-33.650496, in the north-west corner by the shopping centre.
  - About 20 x 13 m.
  - Several blue sails low round the edge and a lime-green diamond sail high in the middle.
  - Blue rubber soft-fall underneath.
- **The lawn:** about 50 x 45 m, irregular, with seating nodes and lamp posts round its edge. The tall trees are along Queen Street and Prince Street.

## Walls and roof

- **Canopies:** PVC fabric, red `#a8343c` and dark green `#2f5e3c`, each with a white `#efefea` panel. Drawn with the edge at about 2.5 m and the top at about 4.8 m (from the shadows).
- **Sails:** blue `#1f6fb5` and `#2a86c8`, lime `#9ccc3c`, about 3-5.6 m up on steel posts.
- **Seat walls:** low rendered walls about 0.45 m high under each canopy. These anchor the spec on the map.

## What a driver sees

From Queen Street and Prince Street, through the street trees: a red and a green fabric canopy at the ends of an open lawn, and a bright blue and lime shade over the playground in the far corner, against the white wall of Busselton Central.

## Unsure

- **Whether the canopies are permanent.** The City's design drawings showed Corten "tree" shelters with hexagon canopies instead. The aerial shows these fabric canopies, so they may be what was built, or event marquees. Worth a look.
- The canopy panels' exact count and shape. Six is drawn.
- Sail heights and the playground equipment under them, which isn't drawn.
