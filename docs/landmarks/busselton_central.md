# Busselton Central: street faces

## What it is

Busselton Central, the town's shopping centre, between Kent St (north) and Prince St (south), west of Mitchell Park and Queen St. A $45 million redevelopment finished in stages from 2023:

- **Anchors:** Coles, Reading Cinemas (five screens on level one, with a Titan Luxe auditorium), Timezone, Cotton On Mega.
- **East side:** an indoor-outdoor dining precinct opening onto Mitchell Park and the plaza off Kent St (Benesse, Inara, Dôme and others).
- **Busselton Pavilion:** the Parker Group's gastropub, wine shop and distillery, about 1,000 m², at the north-east corner (Shop T46, 30 Kent St). Opened January 2024.

**The accepted base builds it as one generic box from its OSM footprint:** about 100 by 95 m, `shop_red` walls to 4 m with `upper_cream` above to about 9 m, generic windows, and a flat zinc roof. A plain awning runs along Prince St, with a shop verandah and blank sign on its eastern 58 m. A thin sliver building fills the gap between the box and the slanted Prince St wall. The box's footprint already has the alfresco notch at the north-east corner and a few points for the cinema's curve (`out/refs/busselton_central/central_walls.jpg`).

**Its centroid is mid-block.** The ring's vertex mean (what the clear test uses) is about 115.34355,-33.65059, pulled north-east by the corner's extra points, but still 29 m from the nearest spec here, so no clear circle takes the box. All four specs are **skins**: they stand against or just in front of its walls, and the box stays as the mass behind them.

**Route:** the Town loop runs down Queen St past the plaza and the Kent St corner. Kent St west of the plaza and Prince St are seen in free roam.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial (Oct 2026): `central.jpg` (centre 115.3430,-33.6505, half 110 m), `necorner.jpg` (115.34375,-33.65045, half 32 m), `kentmid.jpg`, `prince.jpg` and `nw.jpg` in `out/refs/busselton_central/`. The base box's walls were read from the accepted build.

- **Kent St wall:** straight, bearing 76, from 115.342589,-33.650449 to 115.343592,-33.650239 (96 m).
- **North-east corner, west to east:**
  - a square tower about 6 m on the corner
  - the alfresco notch, about 21 by 28 m, with umbrellas over a brick-red deck, a low flat-roofed annex at its back, and a glass grid roof (the stair atrium) in its south-east corner
  - the cinema's rounded white volume, set back about 14 m from the Kent St line behind a paved plaza with planters, and wrapped at the foot by a white finned canopy
- **Kent St, west of the corner:** a long white segmented canopy, about 2.5 m deep, over the footpath for about 50 m. A skylit strip, the mall's north-south spine, meets the wall about 35 m west of the corner. A service yard and loading dock fill the far west end.
- **Prince St:** the wall steps at 115.343509,-33.651208. The spine meets Prince St about 11 m west of the step.

## Walls and colours

From a June 2025 photo from the plaza (Cinema Treasures) and the Pavilion's opening photo (January 2024). Marketing copy calls it "a clean, coastal-inspired colour scheme".

- **Pavilion:** white vertical boards, a black arched entry, black steel truss. BUSSELTON PAVILION in dark capitals on two lines on the tower.
- **Cinema:** a white rendered rounded upper volume with READING CINEMAS in dark capitals, a grey ribbed band on top, and a dark glazed box with the stair beside it. A white wave canopy runs over timber-clad shopfronts.
- **Colours used:**
  - off-white `#EEECE6`
  - timber `#A7774F`
  - charcoal `#2A2C2F`
  - glazing `#2C3438`
  - ink `#2B2A2E`
  - umbrellas from the aerial: terracotta `#D98370` and cream `#EFE6CF`
- **Kent St and Prince St entries:** no street photos of either face were found (Mapillary has nothing there newer than 2019). Their portals and the BUSSELTON CENTRAL lettering are guesses in the same palette.

## What a driver sees

1. **Coming down Queen St (Town loop):** across the plaza, the white cinema volume with READING CINEMAS, its wave canopy, and the Pavilion's tower and umbrellas to the right.
2. **Along Kent St (free roam):** the tower at the corner, then the white board front of the Pavilion hall, the entry portal and the long canopy.
3. **Along Prince St (free roam):** a white portal with BUSSELTON CENTRAL standing out from the existing awning.

**Tris:** 870, 268, 348 and 138.

## What it couldn't represent

- **Pavilion:** the arch, drawn square. The truss's lattice, drawn as plain beams. The striped umbrellas, drawn plain.
- **Cinema:** the curve, drawn as steps. The wave canopy's fins, drawn as flat slabs.
- **Signs:** each faces Kent St or Prince St only, the only way a spec sign can face. Nothing is lettered on the cinema's park side.
- **The rest of the box:** still the generic `shop_red` and `upper_cream` walls with generic windows, wherever no skin covers it: the west face to the car park, Prince St outside the portal, and the cinema's east face south of the canopy.
- **Prince St:** the base's own awning still runs through the portal's piers.

## Unsure

- **The entries:** where they are and what they look like on both streets, read only from the skylit spine in the aerial.
- **The cinema volume:** its height. The photo suggests 12 to 14 m.
- **The annex and atrium:** their heights.
- **The canopy:** whether the Kent St canopy is translucent.
- **Mitchell Park:** its playground sails (`mitchell_park.json`) reach about 0.4 m inside the base box's east wall, near this spec's east canopy. That's an existing overlap, not one this spec adds.
