# Busselton Visitor Centre (Railway House)

## What it is

The Busselton Visitor Centre (Margaret River Busselton Tourism Association) on the jetty foreshore, north of Foreshore Parade. It opened in 2016/17 and is known as **Railway House**.

It is a group of buildings:
- the **old Busselton Railway Station** (built 1894, moved here in mid-2016; on the City's Local Heritage List, Category 2, Oct 2024)
- a **new visitor centre** joined to its east side
- a **new shed** for the jetty train and the Ballaarat steam engine
- a service and toilet block to the south

The jetty train's "Visitor Centre" stop is on the west side, at a rebuilt platform.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial (`out/refs/visitor_centre/aerial40.jpg`, 0.078 m/px) and checked against the OSM outline. The buildings are single storey, so lean is small.

- **Overall:** roughly a 34 x 30 m block, turned so its long edges run on a bearing of 112°/292° (WNW-ESE).
  - North edge: 33.7 m (OSM 33.5 m).
  - East edge: about 30 m. OSM has 30 m in three steps.
  - South edge: about 29 m.
  - West side: stepped, about 22 to 26 m.
  - OSM area is 1,075 m². Mine is a little bigger because it includes the verandah roofs.
- **Roof sections (north to south)**, all low-pitched corrugated metal:
  1. **North roof (shed and hall):** about 33.7 x 12 m, very light grey with **two blocks of solar panels** (about 4 x 4 panels each) on its western half. It is long and plain, possibly a single skillion or a very low gable. This is most likely the jetty-train and Ballaarat shed.
  2. **Middle roof:** a darker grey strip about 6 to 7 m wide, running the full length. A black gutter or valley line is along its south side.
  3. **South roofs:** a light verandah or ridge strip, then a blue-grey roof about 28 m long and 7 m wide. Its eaves are on the south side. This is probably the old station and its platform verandah. The photo shows the station gable end facing east.
  4. **East end:** small white flat roofs and a fenced **rooftop plant area** (about 7 x 6 m) at the south-east corner, behind a white louvred screen.
- **Faces:** the public front faces **SSE onto Foreshore Parade** (bearing about 160°). A verandah with dark fascia runs along the east part. The north side faces the Busselton Jetty car park, behind a kerb and garden strip. The jetty-train track comes in from the west: one line curves to the station platform on the south-west, and one line to the north-west corner of the shed.
- **Setback:** the south face is about 10 to 14 m from the north kerb of Foreshore Parade, across a paved plaza with bins, benches and a bus bay. This is ±3 m: the kerb line is partly hidden by a parked bus in the aerial.
- **Centre (lng,lat):** **115.345741,-33.645068** (middle of the measured block). OSM centroid: 115.345742,-33.645055.

## Walls and roof

- **Old station (south-west part, seen from Foreshore Parade):**
  - Single-storey weatherboard, painted **white**, about `#f2f1ec`. Corrugated metal gable roof in light grey (`#c8ccc8`), about 30° pitch.
  - Eaves about 3.2 m, ridge about 6 m (estimated).
  - The **east gable end faces the road** at an angle, with an external face-brick **chimney** in the middle (red-brown brick, about `#8a4f3e`), standing about 1 m above the ridge.
  - A curved (bullnose) white verandah awning is on the south side, left of the gable as seen from the east. The west side has the original verandah with timber posts and decorative brackets, the curved ticket windows and the rebuilt platform.
  - A "Welcome to Railway House" interpretive board stands next to the chimney.
- **New visitor centre (east part):**
  - Single storey with white or very light grey horizontal-board walls (`#eceeed`) and a low-pitched light-grey roof.
  - A **verandah** runs along the front, about 2.5 to 3 m deep, on slim dark posts. The fascia is dark red-brown (about `#5a2e2a`; unconfirmed, the photo is backlit).
  - Above the verandah, at its west end, is a **white horizontal louvre screen** about 1.5 m tall around the rooftop plant, with a dark cylindrical vent poking up.
  - **Street face, left to right:** a glazed entrance bay, dark glass doors and windows under the verandah, a white wall with a small sign plate, a large window, then a low rendered garden wall and planter (`#d8cfb5`) in front of the east end.
- **North shed:** the long north roof. Walls not seen in any photo. It is probably metal-clad, light colour.
- **Signage:** the "Visitor Centre" name is not legible in the 2021 street photos. Assume plain lettering on the verandah fascia over the entrance.

## Grounds

- **South (Foreshore Parade side):**
  - A wide pale paved plaza (limestone-coloured, `#d9c8a8`) with a line of black wheelie bins, two information pylons and street lights carrying banners.
  - A raised rendered planter with native shrubs at the south-east corner.
  - A bus bay on Foreshore Parade's north side, right in front.
- **West:** paved plaza and garden beds. A shade sail or pergola structure (yellow slats) stands over a planter, and the train track crosses the plaza.
- **North:** the Busselton Jetty car park (angle bays), with tall palms along the kerb and a low garden strip against the building.
- **Neighbours:**
  - The Goose (café, north-west).
  - Shelter Brewing Co (dark charcoal gabled building, south-west), with "BORN & BREWED IN BUSSELTON" on its wall. From the east, it is directly behind the station.

## What a driver sees

1. A small **white weatherboard railway station** with a **red-brick chimney** on its gable end and a curved verandah, set back across a paved plaza.
2. A long, low **white building** with a verandah, a white louvred screen on the roof and a dark vent, east of the station.
3. Pale paved plaza, bins and banner poles, with a bus bay at the kerb.
4. The **jetty train track** crossing the plaza to the west, with Shelter Brewing's dark barn behind.
5. From above or from the jetty side: a big, plain light-grey roof with solar panels.

## Unsure

- **Which roof is which:** I matched them by reasoning, not by a labelled plan. I'm fairly sure about the station being the south roof and the shed being the north roof, but not certain.
- **Station orientation and length:** about 28 m measured. That is long for the station alone and may include the joined visitor centre.
- **Colours:** the only street photos are backlit dashcam images from January 2021. Trim colours are uncertain.
- **The north and west faces:** no photos found. Wall heights of the shed are unknown (OSM `render_height=5`).
