# Orana Cinemas

## What it is

Orana Cinemas Busselton, the town's multiplex. It is on the north side of **Albert St (27 Albert St)**, a few doors west of the Queen St corner. The building does not front Queen St. From the Queen St lights it is the tall pale box standing above the two-storey shops on the corner. OSM puts the cinema at 115.344500,-33.652025.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerials (`out/refs/orana_cinemas/aerial50.jpg` and `aerial30.jpg`, 0.098 and 0.059 m/px) and checked against the OSM outline. The building is tall, so the aerial shows lean and a long shadow. I measured roof edges, not wall feet.

- **Axes:** the long walls run on a bearing of about 348°/168°. The front faces **SSE onto Albert St, bearing about 167°**.
- **Front auditorium block:** 20.4 m wide x 24 m deep. A flat grey-green roof (Colorbond, about `#8f9a92`) with a faint ridge down the middle, behind a pale parapet. It is the taller part.
- **Rear auditorium block:** 17.5 m wide x 22 m deep, lined up with the west wall. The east side steps in about 3 m. It is lower, the same grey-green roof.
- **Rooftop plant:** a plant deck (about 5.5 x 3.3 m) and a row of condensers on the rear block, against the front block's back wall.
- **Overall length:** about 46 m (OSM 44.5 m).
- **Canopy:** an awning about 3.6 m deep along the front, over the footpath.
- **Neighbours:**
  - West, attached: a narrow cream hip-roofed building (about 7 x 20 m). It is not part of the cinema, and the generated base builds it.
  - East: a car park, then the two-storey office and shop block on the Queen St corner.
  - North: a large public car park.
- **Centre (lng,lat):** **115.344454,-33.651880** (middle of both blocks).

## Walls and roof

From two October 2019 Mapillary photos, westbound on Albert St:

- **Height:** about 11 m, clearly taller than the two-storey corner block. The rear block is about 9.5 m (estimated).
- **Walls:** pale beige render panels with vertical joints, about `#d8cfbd`.
- **Front, ground floor:** a base of **sandstone-coloured block cladding** (about `#c6a878`) with glass entrance doors and lit poster cases, under a **dark teal-grey canopy** (about `#3e5552`).
- **East end of the front:** the stone cladding rises as a tall pier, about 5 m wide and 8 m high, and wraps a few metres round the corner.
- **Name:** **"ORANA CINEMAS"** in dark grey plain capitals, high on the **east side wall** near the front, in two lines, readable from the Queen St corner.

## Grounds

- **Front:** the Albert St footpath, red brick paving (about `#a35a45`), with a street light, a bin and a bus stop sign.
- **Albert St:** a divided road here, with a red-paved median and kerb lanes. Across the road: the BP station, and further east the War Memorial at the Queen St corner.
- **Back and sides:** asphalt car parks.

## What a driver sees

1. Coming west from Queen St: a **plain, tall beige box** standing above the corner shops, with "ORANA CINEMAS" high on its side.
2. Then a **stone-clad corner pier**, and a **dark canopy** over the entrance and poster cases along the footpath.
3. From the north car park: two big plain rendered boxes, the rear one lower, with plant on the roof.

## Unsure

- **The sign:** in the photos the name is on the east side wall. Spec signs can only face the front, so the spec puts the name on the front facade, which may not match the real front. Signs added since 2019 are unknown.
- **The front above the canopy:** seen only obliquely, from the east, at 34 to 80 m.
- **Block heights:** estimated from the photos and shadows, ±1 m.
- **Entrance layout:** door width and position under the canopy are guessed.
- **Rear block walls:** not seen. Assumed to be the same render, slightly darker.
- **Photo age:** the photos are from 2019. Paint and signage may have changed.
