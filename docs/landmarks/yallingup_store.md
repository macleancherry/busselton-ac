# Yallingup General Store

## What it is

Yallingup's shop and post office, on the corner by the Caves Road / Yallingup Beach Road intersection, across the car park from Caves House. OSM tags it "Yallingup Post Office". The Yallingup precinct heritage listing names "Caves, Caves House, Store, Hall, Power House and Farm" and describes timber-framed buildings of about 1912. Neighbours: the Bottlemart in a small red-tiled house to the west, and the Gugelhupf bakery in a flat-roofed shop to the south-east.

Route: **caves-road.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/caves_road_yallingup_looks.json`.

## Walls and roof

- Cream weatherboard `#EFE7D2` (assumed).
- Pale corrugated roofs `#E2E1D6` (the aerial reads near white).
- Dark green trim `#2F4F3E` (assumed).
- Big shop windows either side of the door.
- Signs in plain lettering: "GENERAL STORE" on the verandah fascia, a small red "POST OFFICE" board.

## What a driver sees

The route is 204 m east on Caves Road. From the intersection with Yallingup Beach Road, a low cream shop with a verandah under big peppermints, the car park in front.
