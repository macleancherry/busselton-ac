# Margaret River Visitor Centre

## What it is

The region's main visitor centre, 100 Bussell Hwy, on the south-west corner of Bussell Hwy and Tunbridge St, at the north end of the shops. Not heritage listed.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_vc/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.07402,-33.94885, half 50 m, so 0.0977 m/px; scratchpad copy `mr/vc.jpg`). About ±1 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **east onto Bussell Hwy** (bearing about 96°). The north side faces a paved corner forecourt and the Tunbridge St junction.
- **Middle:** **115.073967, -33.948951**.
- **North block:** about 17 m along the street by 22.5 m deep, two storeys. Low hip roof with deep eaves and a cross ridge towards the corner, solar panels on the north slope.
- **South block:** about 16 m along the street by 23.5 m deep, set about 4.4 m back, single storey (tall). Hip roof with a second solar array.
- A car park on the west side, off Tunbridge St; a big shed-roofed retail building directly south.

## Walls and roof

From 2019 street photos (Mapillary). Hex values are approximate.

- **Lower walls:** rust-red render `#86412E`.
- **Upper floor (north block):** a band of dark glazing `#2B3236` in grey frames, under a sand-coloured fascia band.
- **Roof:** Colorbond in a grey-green `#46594F`, low pitch (about 24°), deep eaves.
- **Map board:** a large pale regional map panel on the front wall.
- **South block:** a pale green Colorbond verandah on posts over the footpath.
- **Signage:** "Visitor Centre" in plain lettering. No logo.

## Grounds

- Red brick footpath along Bussell Hwy; pale paved forecourt on the north corner, with planting and a sculpture.
- Street trees along the kerb.

## What a driver sees

Coming into town from the north, past the bend at Tunbridge St, it's the first big building on the right:

1. The grey-green hip roof with deep eaves and the glazed upper floor.
2. The rust-red walls and the big map board.
3. The forecourt on the corner.

## Unsure

- Roof colour: green in the street photos, darker grey-teal in the aerial. Could be Colorbond Cottage Green or Woodland Grey.
- Whether the upper floor runs the whole north block or only the street side.
- The 2019 photos are seven years old; the front may have been refreshed since.
