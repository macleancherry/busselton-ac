# Augusta Hotel bottle shop

## What it is

The north end of the Augusta Hotel on Blackwood Avenue, just behind the start of the Cape Leeuwin route:

- the drive-in bottle shop, with its drive-through canopy off a sealed forecourt;
- the public and lounge bars;
- the hotel's two-storey cream block behind a single-storey verandah.

Route: **cape-leeuwin.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the same aerials and frame as `augusta_hotel.md` (x along the road from the route's first point, y back from the building line).

- **Bottle shop:** 16 x 6.5 m, 20 to 36 m before the start, with a verandah in front. A 5 m drive-through canopy over the forecourt is at its north end.
- **Two-storey block:** 13 x 10 m, 7 to 20 m before the start, behind a verandah.
- **Rear:** 16 x 11.6 m, single storey, behind the bottle shop.
- Middle **115.160349, -34.316660** (the spec's centre).
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **248°**.

## Walls and roof

- Cream render, red Colorbond hips (manorred).
- A raised fascia with "DRIVE-IN BOTTLE SHOP" and "PUBLIC BAR & LOUNGE" in plain dark lettering on cream. The Bottlemart brand board is left off.
- A low rendered planter wall with red brick capping along the footpath, open at the drive-in.

## What a driver sees

Looking back from the grid, or in the mirrors, the red-roofed bottle shop and the two-storey block next to the hotel.

## Approximations

- The two-storey block's roof is a hip. It may be gabled to the street.
- The drive-through is an open canopy on posts.

## Unsure

- Where the drive-through enters and how deep it goes.
