# Collie Motorplex clubrooms

## What it is

The long, low building with the khaki roof beside the race tower, on the paddock side of the main straight. Its use isn't confirmed: it is most likely the clubrooms or canteen. Spec: `routes/landmarks/collie_clubrooms.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial, rotated 38° (`colliem/tower_rot.jpg`). It sits about 9° off the straight. About ±0.6 m.

- **Orientation:** long axis bearing about 47°, front **facing the track (316.8°)**.
- **Middle:** **116.243686, -33.430225**.
- **Size:** about 26.5 x 9.5 m, single storey.
- **Setback:** about 26 m from the track edge.
- **Round canopies:** two round white shade canopies at its south end, about 4 m and 4.8 m across, and a small dark one.

## Walls and roof

- **Roof:** near-flat Colorbond in khaki `#C7BD82`, with an overhang on posts along the track side (modelled 1.8 m).
- **Walls:** not seen in any photo. Modelled as cream cladding `#D9CFB6` with windows along both long sides.
- **Height:** about 3.2 m.
- **Signage:** none modelled.

## Grounds

Concrete under the overhang, tables under the round canopies, scrub and a couple of trees along the north and east sides, and a car park to the east.

## What a driver sees

A long, low khaki roof just before the race tower on the left of the main straight, partly behind the wall line and trees.

## Unsure

- What the building is (clubrooms, canteen or scrutineering) and its wall finish.
- Whether the round white things are shade canopies or water tanks. They are modelled as canopies (umbrellas).
