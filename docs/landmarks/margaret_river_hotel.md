# Margaret River Hotel

## What it is

The town's first official hotel, 125 Bussell Hwy, on the south-east corner of Bussell Hwy and Willmott Ave. Built from 1936 (architect F G B Hawkins, builder C Arnott) for Bernard McKeown. Motel units were added out the back in 1970 and 1972, the front bar and lounge were extended in 1970, and a large addition in the same style went up in 1985. State Register since 2008, National Trust classified 1995. The Heytesbury Group bought it in 2021 and is redeveloping it.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_hotel/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.07407,-33.95142, half 60 m, so 0.117 m/px; scratchpad copy `mr/hotel.jpg`). Roof outlines include eaves, so walls are about 0.4 m in from them. Two storeys, so about ±1 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **west onto Bussell Hwy** (bearing about 272°). The north end faces Willmott Ave.
- **Middle of the 1936 building:** **115.074004, -33.951413**.
- **Main range:** about 42.5 m along Bussell Hwy by 11.6 m deep, set about 8 m back from the front of the south wing. Gable roof with the ridge along the street, gable ends north and south.
- **Street gables (west side), north to south:**
  - a narrow two-storey gable bay, about 6 m wide, near the north end
  - the entry bay, about 9.4 m wide, projecting about 5.7 m, with the single-storey gabled entry porch in front of it
  - the south wing, about 10 m wide by 14 m deep, its front about 0.4 m behind the footpath line
- **1985 wing:** a single-storey hipped block behind the south end, about 14 x 13.5 m.
- **Rear yard:** the old motel units and beer garden were cleared; the October 2026 aerial shows a building site with footings over most of the lot east of the hotel. Not modelled.

## Walls and roof

From 2019 street photos (Mapillary) and the inHerit record. Hex values are approximate.

- **Walls:** rendered brick, warm taupe `#8E8272`.
- **Gables:** Inter-War Old English half-timber look: white barge boards and a white frame `#F0EEE6` round a brown panel `#7A6C5C`, with white vertical battens. A white string course at first-floor level on the bays.
- **Roof:** terracotta tiles, `#9C4A3A` (reads `#A5463E` in the aerial). Pitch about 38-45°.
- **Chimney:** one tall rendered chimney near the north end, rising well above the ridge; a second, shorter one mid-roof.
- **Windows:** white-framed double-hung windows, many with shutters, upstairs and down.
- **Entry porch:** single storey on slim white posts, with a gable front. A small hanging sign by the porch.
- **Signage:** "Margaret River Hotel" in plain lettering. No logo.

## Grounds

- Red brick footpath along Bussell Hwy; a low rendered wall and clipped hedge along the front lawn north of the entry.
- In 2019 a glazed white-framed beer garden pavilion with white shade umbrellas stood at the north-west corner. The 2026 aerial shows that corner bare; not modelled until it's rebuilt.
- Street trees in the footpath.

## What a driver sees

Driving south down the hill from the shops, it's the last big building on the left before Willmott Ave:

1. The terracotta roofs and the tall chimney against the sky.
2. Three gables to the street with white barges and battens, two storeys of white-framed windows.
3. The little gabled entry porch with the hotel sign.

## Unsure

- What the Heytesbury redevelopment keeps or adds. The 1936 building is heritage listed, so it should stay, but new buildings will go up behind and maybe on the north corner. Recheck the aerial before polishing.
- The exact pitch and size of each gable; trees hide parts of the west side.
- The north gable end of the main range has the same half-timbering in the photos, but the spec's pediments only face the street, so it shows a plain gable there.
