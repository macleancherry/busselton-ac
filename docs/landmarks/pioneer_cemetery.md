# Busselton Pioneer Cemetery

## What it is

The Old Busselton Cemetery, called the Busselton Pioneer Cemetery in OSM. It is the block bounded by Marine Tce (north), Stanley St (west) and Adelaide St (south), with the Kookaburra Caravan Park to the east. It opened in 1847, was gazetted in 1856 and is on the State Register.

- The 1996 heritage survey recorded about 250 headstones. The City's burial list has over 1,000 likely burials.
- The City cleared the overgrowth about 2023. It also added interpretive signs and a map, new granite name tablets and a memorial rock for Samuel Isaacs.

## Footprint

From the OSM boundary (way 588019005, `barrier=fence`) and the Landgate aerial `out/refs/pioneer_cemetery/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.34682,-33.64670, half 70 m, so 0.137 m/px). Close-ups: `aerial_north.jpg` (Marine Tce edge) and `aerial_sw.jpg` (entrance corner), both half 25 m.

- **Shape and size:** about 120 m along Adelaide St and 99 m from Marine Tce to Adelaide St. Its edges run square to the town grid: the street fronts face 346° and 166°, the side edges 76° and 256°.
  - North-east cut-out: a house lot about 40 m by 50 m, with a green-roofed house and a shed, between the Marine Tce frontage and the caravan park.
  - Corner cuts: about 13 m at Marine Tce and Stanley St, and about 11 m at Stanley St and Adelaide St.
- **Marine Tce frontage:** about 72 m, from the house lot to the Stanley St corner. A thick line of trees and shrubs runs inside the fence.
- **Entrance:** OSM puts it at the Stanley St and Adelaide St corner (node `barrier=entrance`). The close-up shows a short wall or structure there.
- **Graves:** loose clusters on grey sand and sparse grass between big peppermints and tuarts. Many are kerbed plots about 1 by 2.2 m, some with headstones and white crosses. The biggest clusters are:
  - north-west and north-middle
  - the middle of the west half
  - south-west of the middle
  - a long group in the south-middle
  - the south-east
- **Spec anchor:** the entrance pillars, at **115.346398, -33.647204**. The middle of the whole block is about 115.346796, -33.646664.

## Walls, fences and graves

From the City's 2023 photo of the entrance path (`bm_0.jpg`) and the aerial. Hex values are approximate.

- **Fence:** OSM tags the boundary a fence. The street sides are drawn as a low dark steel fence, about 1.2 m, `#2A2C2C`. The caravan park and house-lot sides are drawn as grey Colorbond, 1.8 m. The aerial shows a hard line along the house lot.
- **Entrance:** two limestone pillars with caps `#DDD3BA` and a pair of steel gates. Inside, a path of large pale concrete pavers `#CFCABF` with red brick edging `#9B5A48`. Grey steel name-tablet panels stand either side on short legs, angled in life and upright here.
- **Graves:**
  - headstones in white marble `#E8E6E0`, some in sandstone `#C9B79A` or grey granite `#8D8A85`, 0.7 to 1.1 m tall
  - kerbed plots and ledger slabs, low
  - white marble crosses on small bases, about 1.4 m
  - all lying east-west, headstone at the west end
- **Ground:** pale grey sand with patchy grass in summer, green lawn in winter (`bm_0.jpg`).

## What a driver sees

1. **Along Marine Tce:** a low dark fence behind a dense line of trees and shrubs. White headstones and crosses glint between the trunks.
2. **Down Stanley St:** the open grounds behind the fence, big trees, and graves scattered in clusters.
3. **At the Adelaide St corner:** the pillars and gates, the paved path, and the grey tablet panels.

## How the spec draws it

`routes/landmarks/pioneer_cemetery.json`, generated by the scratchpad `lm3/gen_cem.py`.

- **Main blocks:** only the two gate pillars, so `at` is the entrance.
- **Fences:** run along the spec axes, so the two corner cuts are stepped. The steps stay inside the boundary by up to about 4 m.
- **Graves:** about 70 low panels in the aerial's clusters (seeded random, so rebuilds don't change). About 3,800 triangles in all; the open fences' posts are about half of that.
- **No lettered sign:** the name on site isn't known, and a sign in specHouse can only face Marine Tce.
- **No trees:** OSM has 12 tree nodes here and the aerial shows about 40 crowns; whatever the build already plants is left as it is.

## Unsure

- **The fence.** Its type and colour aren't confirmed by any photo. There is no Mapillary coverage here.
- **The entrance.** Whether it is on the corner itself, on Stanley St or on Adelaide St, and whether it has pillars and gates at all. Whether Marine Tce has a gate too.
- **The name on any sign.** It could be "Old Busselton Cemetery" or "Pioneer Cemetery".
- **Ground level.** The whole spec sits at one ground height. Over 120 m that may leave some graves floating or sunk a little if the ground isn't flat.
