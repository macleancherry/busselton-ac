# Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse

## What it is

The lighthouse on the bluff at the end of Cape Naturaliste Road, about 100 m above Geographe Bay. Built from February to December 1903 and first lit on 15 April 1904. The light is 123 m above sea level, so the tower itself can be short. It is part of the State-registered "Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse and Quarters" (8 ha reserve, next to Leeuwin-Naturaliste National Park). Guided tours climb the tower; the keepers' cottages are a separate landmark (`cape_naturaliste_cottages`).

Route: **cape-naturaliste.json** (Cape Naturaliste Road). Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_naturaliste_road_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_lighthouse/nat.jpg` (centre 115.0191,-33.5373, half 120 m) and `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_lighthouse/nlh.jpg` (centre on the tower, half 15 m), plus OSM.

- **Tower:** round, about 7.5 m across at the base, narrowing a little to the gallery. Middle at **115.018705, -33.537391** (the aerial leans the top a few metres; OSM has 115.01874,-33.53736).
- **Gallery:** a moulded concrete balcony round the top of the shaft, about 8 m across, with an iron balustrade (aluminium mesh added).
- **Lantern:** cast-iron Chance lantern, about 4.3 m (14 ft) across, diamond-paned glazing, domed cap with a ventilator.
- **Power house and store (1973):** small flat-roofed brick building about 6 x 5 m just east of the tower.
- **Enclosure:** the tower stands in a fenced compound (cyclone mesh) on the highest point, a sealed path running about 170 m south to the cottages and the car park.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **165°**, down the path towards the cottages.

## Walls and roof

- Tower: coursed, quarry-faced limestone with dressed edges round the plinth and openings, painted white all over `#F1F0EA`.
- Gallery and balustrade: white `#EFEEE8`.
- Lantern: white frame, dark glazing `#26313A`, white dome.
- Small windows up the tower, one above another; a door at the foot facing the path.
- Power house: red-brown brick `#8C4A36`, pale flat roof.

## What a driver sees

Cape Naturaliste Road ends about 900 m short of the precinct (see Unsure), so as routed it is a white tower top over the heath from the last stretch. Driven to the car park, it's a short, fat white tower with a big lantern on the crest above the cottages.

## Approximations

- **Round tower:** specHouse only has boxes. Each tier is two crossed bars and a square (a stepped twelve-sided outline), two tiers narrowing upwards. Same for the gallery (three overlapping slabs), the lantern pedestal, the glazing and the dome (a steep hip on the square).
- **Balustrade:** solid white rail panels round the stepped outline, not open ironwork.

**Wants a Blender layer:** a true round, tapered tower; the corbelled gallery and open balustrade; the lantern's diamond glazing, astragals and dome.
