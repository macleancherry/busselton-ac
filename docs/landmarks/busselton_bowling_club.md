# Busselton Bowling Club

## What it is

The Busselton Bowling Club, 24 Brown Street, on the south-west corner of the Churchill Park sporting grounds. Bowls WA's Country Club of the Year in 2018. A long single-storey clubhouse on the north side of four bowling greens, with a car park behind it off Brown Street.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/parks/bowls.jpg` (centre 115.3497,-33.6485, 0.107 m/px) and checked against the OSM outline of the club grounds. Single storey, so lean is small. About ±0.7 m.

- **Orientation:** everything sits on the grid of Brown Street and the greens. The long axis runs about 76.5°/256.5°. The clubhouse front faces the greens, bearing about 166.5° (SSE).
- **Middle of the clubhouse:** **115.349885, -33.648262**.
- **Clubhouse, west to east:**
  - **West wing:** about 11.5 x 13 m, lower hipped roof.
  - **Hall:** about 40 x 15.5 m, hipped roof with its ridge along the building. Two blocks of solar panels on the north slope.
  - **Kitchen and store rooms:** about 5 x 14.5 m of small flat white roofs at the east end, with a ramp and a pedestrian crossing to the car park on the north-east.
  - **Verandah:** a white flat roof about 2.2 m deep along the whole front of the west wing and hall, about 51 m.
- **Greens:** four greens, each about 34 x 34 m. The two main greens lie directly in front of the clubhouse, side by side. The other two are to the west, by the Brown Street corner. A concrete path about 4 m wide runs between the verandah and the greens.
- **Shelters:** two long green-roofed seating shelters along the south edge of the main greens, each about 25.5 x 3.2 m, backed onto the street.
- **Setback:** the verandah is about 43 m north of the boundary fence on the south street. Tall light poles stand at the corners of the greens.

## Walls and roof

From the aerial only. I found no clear street-level photo (Mapillary has none within 300 m). Hex values are approximate.

- **Roof:** pale green Colorbond, `#9fbfa4` in the aerial. The verandah and the east rooms are white or off-white. **The build has no green roof colour** (ROOF_COLOURS has no Colorbond greens), so it comes out as the nearest grey (zinc). The Blender render uses the true colour.
- **Walls:** not visible from above. Drawn as cream face brick `#d8c9ac` with a row of big windows to the greens. **Guess.**
- **Height:** walls about 3.2-3.4 m. Hall ridge about 6 m.
- **Signage:** drawn as "BUSSELTON BOWLING CLUB", plain lettering on a board above the verandah facing the greens. **The real sign's place and style are unknown.**

## Grounds

- **Greens:** ground from the base's aerial, not modelled.
- **Shelters:** green Colorbond back walls to the street, flat green roofs, open to the greens on slim posts, with bench seating under them.
- **Fence:** low black wire fence along the south boundary. There is a hedge or windbreak between the west greens and the main greens.
- **Car park:** asphalt behind the clubhouse with planted islands, shared with the Churchill Park grounds.

## What a driver sees

From the street to the south, across the shelters and the two main greens:

1. A long low clubhouse with a pale green hipped roof and a white verandah strip along its front.
2. Two long green shelters right on the footpath.
3. Light poles at the corners of the greens.

From the car park side: the plain back of the hall, the solar panels and the white roofs of the store rooms.

## Unsure

- Wall material and colour, and where the name sign is.
- Whether the shelters have back walls or are open both sides. The aerial shows a dark line along the street side.
- The roof colour: it may be Colorbond Pale Eucalypt or an older Mist Green.
