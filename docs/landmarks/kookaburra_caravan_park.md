# Kookaburra Caravan Park (now the Busselton Jetty Tourist Park)

## What it is

The caravan park on the block between Marine Tce (north), Brown St (east) and Adelaide St (south), with the Pioneer Cemetery to the west. OSM way 189696741 (`tourism=caravan_site`); the address is 2 Brown St, on the corner of Marine Tce.

- Holidaymakers have camped here since the early 20th century. From the 1920s to the 1950s, Midland Railway workshop families came down by special train every Christmas. Caravans came from the 1960s.
- The Shire ran it, then leased it out. For many years it was the Kookaburra Caravan Park. Since about 2024 it is the **Busselton Jetty Tourist Park**, owned by the City and run by park managers. Category 4 on the City's Local Heritage Survey.
- The Tourist Park is three parks. This block is **Park 1** on the City's 2026 park map.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerials `kk_a.jpg` (centre 115.34765,-33.64645, half 70 m), `kk_z.jpg` (115.34775,-33.64605, half 35 m, 0.068 m/px) and `kk_r.jpg` (115.34815,-33.64600, half 20 m, 0.039 m/px), in `out/refs/kookaburra_caravan_park/`, with the 2026 park map. Single storey, so lean is small. About ±0.5 m.

- **Orientation:** Marine Tce here runs at about 80°, so the front faces **350°**. The buildings sit square to it.
- **Frontage:** the Marine Tce footpath, then a mown lawn about 3 m deep. No fence shows in the aerial. Big peppermints between the buildings.
- **Reception:** on the Marine Tce and Brown St corner, as the park map says. Roof about **15.1 m by 9.4 m**, pale grey-green metal, nearly flat in the aerial (a low hip). Middle about **115.348063, -33.646014**.
  - A concrete path comes in from the Marine Tce footpath along its west end.
  - A green walled bin enclosure, about 4.5 m by 2 m, stands in front of its west half.
- **Front-row cabins:** four white roofs west of the reception, the cabins the park map numbers 6, 7, 11 and 12 (east to west). Each is about 5 to 7 m across the front by 10 to 11 m deep, with its long side running back from the street. They are 9 to 14 m apart. Between cabins 6 and 7 is a small structure, about 2.5 by 7 m, probably a caravan annexe; it is left out.
- **The rest of Park 1:** about 70 sites in rows on lawn under peppermints, more cabins (9, 10, 4 and 5 in the middle rows), internal roads off Brown St, and the amenities block and camp kitchen at the west end. The "house and shed" in the north-east cut-out of the cemetery block are those two: the amenities block has the skylights.

## Walls and roof

From the City's cabin photos (`p1..p4.jpg`), the City's EOI photo (`eoi.png`) and the aerial. Hex values are approximate.

- **Cabins:**
  - white horizontal Colorbond-style cladding `#EEF0EC` with white trim
  - white or Surfmist roofs `#E6E8E4` (very bright in the aerial), low gables or skillions
  - a verandah on two slim white posts with a dark timber deck `#6F5442` or grey tiles, raised a step off the lawn
  - a sliding glass door or a security-screen door, and aluminium windows
- **Reception:** walls not seen in any photo. Drawn in cream render `#E4DCCB`, like the cream block amenities building in the EOI photo, under a pale grey-green roof `#B9C0BA`.
- **Signage:** the park's logo reads "BUSSELTON JETTY tourist park". The spec has a plain navy board, "BUSSELTON JETTY TOURIST PARK", in white letters, on two posts by the corner. No logo.

## What a driver sees

Along Marine Tce, east of the cemetery and opposite the foreshore:

1. A mown lawn behind the footpath, with big peppermints.
2. A row of small white cabins end-on to the street, each with a verandah and deck.
3. On the Brown St corner, the low reception building with its bin enclosure, and the park sign.

## How the spec draws it

`routes/landmarks/kookaburra_caravan_park.json`, generated by the scratchpad `kook/gen_kook.py`. About 870 triangles.

- **Reception:** one low-hip block, 10° pitch, 3 m walls, with a glass door near the west end, three front windows, a concrete apron and the path in.
- **Cabins:** each a gable block with the ridge running back from the street (specHouse puts it along the longer side), 2.5 m walls, 14° pitch, and a 2.4 m verandah with posts and a timber deck on the street end. A door and a window face the street, and one window faces the side.
- **Bin enclosure:** three green Colorbond fences, 1.5 m.
- **Trees:** three peppermints, standing in for the many the aerial shows.
- Walls are drawn at the measured roof outlines, so they sit up to the eaves (about 0.3 to 0.6 m) further out than in life.

## Unsure

- **The reception's walls, door and windows.** No photo of it. Colour, material, where the door is and whether it has a verandah are guesses.
- **Which end of each cabin the verandah is on.** The photos show both side and end verandahs. The street end is a guess that suits the view from the road.
- **The sign.** Its place, size and wording on site. It may still say "Kookaburra Caravan Park", or be on Brown St at the entrance.
- **A frontage fence.** None shows in the aerial; a low post-and-wire fence could be hidden.
- Which cabin is which number. The park map is "indicative only".

## Also noted

- **Pioneer Cemetery spec:** its grey Colorbond fence around the "house lot" in the north-east cut-out is really the boundary between the cemetery and this park's amenities block and camp kitchen. The fence line is right; only the reason was wrong.
