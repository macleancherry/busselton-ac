# NAB building (former Goldsbrough Mort), Margaret River

## What it is

The two-storey building with the red roof on the west side of Bussell Hwy, south of Willmott Ave. It was built in 1952-53 for the stock agents Goldsbrough Mort, was later the TAB, and is now NAB and offices (Main Street Historic Walk site 44). Not heritage listed.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/mr_nab/aerial.jpg` (centre 115.0733,-33.9519, half 30 m, so 0.0586 m/px; overlay `ov.jpg`) and OSM way 1269021004. About ±0.7 m.

- **Orientation:** the front faces **east onto Bussell Hwy (98°)**.
- **Middle:** **115.073415, -33.951862**.
- **Front range:** two storeys, 16.3 m along the street by 10 m deep.
- **North wing:** two storeys, 7.5 m wide, running back to about 24.5 m.
- **Rear:** a single-storey flat-roofed part between them.

## Walls and roof

From 2019 street photos (Mapillary 947705672683577). Hex values are approximate.

- **Roof:** red Colorbond hip `#8B3A32`.
- **Upper floor:** cream `#E6DCC4`, with a long band of white-framed windows (an enclosed upper verandah).
- **Ground floor:** glazed shopfronts under a deep awning with a red fascia.
- **Sign:** "NAB" in plain lettering. No logo.

## What a driver sees

Past the hotel, heading for the Wallcliffe Rd roundabout, it's on the right: the one two-storey red-roofed building in the south half of the shops.

## Clears

It clears only OSM way 1269021004, its own footprint. Its south neighbour (way 1269021003) comes within 1.8 m, so the spec starts 0.2 m north of the shared line.
