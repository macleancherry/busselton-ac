# Cape Naturaliste keepers' cottages

## What it is

The three lighthouse keepers' quarters of 1904, about 170 m south of the Cape Naturaliste tower, beside the visitor car park. Part of the State-registered "Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse and Quarters". Today the eastern cottage holds the Lighthouse Tea Rooms and shop; another is the museum (with the old Jarman Island lens), another the guides' office.

Route: **cape-naturaliste.json**. Looks file: `routes/landmarks/cape_naturaliste_road_looks.json`.

## Footprint

From the Landgate aerial `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_cottages/ncott.jpg` (centre 115.0193,-33.5390, half 50 m), `out/refs/cape_naturaliste_cottages/nat_wide.jpg` and the OSM building outlines.

- **Three cottages in a row** running bearing about 119° (west-north-west to east-south-east), about 29 m apart. Each is about 14.5 m square over its enclosed verandahs, turned about 29° off north to match the row.
  - West: 115.019091, -33.538950
  - Middle: **115.019367, -33.539073** (the spec's centre)
  - East (tea rooms): 115.019637, -33.539202
- **Laundries:** a detached stone laundry and toilet behind each.
- **Setting:** peppermint and coastal scrub all round; the car park and tea-room terrace to the north-east; the path up to the tower to the north.
- **Facing:** the spec's front faces bearing **29°** (towards the car park and paths).

## Walls and roof

From the inHerit description:

- Random coursed limestone, painted, cream-white `#EFE9DA`.
- Hipped roofs of corrugated metal, grey `#C4C7C4`.
- Verandahs enclosed with cement sheet on a timber frame (`#E9E4D4`), under low skillions. Residence 1's east verandah was enclosed as a kitchen.
- Rendered chimneys.
- Dark green trim `#3F5A4A` (assumed).

## What a driver sees

Three low white cottages with grey hip roofs half-hidden in the peppermints as you pull into the car park, the tea rooms nearest.

## Approximations

- The enclosed verandahs are lower blocks on three sides (front and both ends) with near-flat hips.
- Sign: "LIGHTHOUSE TEA ROOMS", plain lettering on a board by the east cottage.
