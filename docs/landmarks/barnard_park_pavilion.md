# Barnard Park Pavilion

## What it is

The Barnard Park Sports Pavilion, opened in 2017, stands between the Barnard Park West and Barnard Park East ovals, on the foreshore east of the jetty. Geographe Bay Road is to the north and a car park road runs down its west side. It holds changerooms, toilets and a function room. The cricket, hockey and football clubs use it. The parks themselves are ground and trees.

## Footprint

- **Orientation:** the long axis runs about 165°/345° (NNW-SSE), along the car park road. The long sides face west (255°) to the car park and east to Barnard Park East.
- **Middle:** **115.350351, -33.644467**.
- **Roof outline (including the verandah):** about 47.5 x 22 m.
- **Walls:** about 41.5 x 16 m. A verandah about 2.7 m wide runs all the way round under the same roof.
- **Roof:**
  - a long hipped main roof with small gablets at the north and south ends
  - a cross gable about 10 m wide on each long side, about 14-24 m from the north end, over the function room
  - solar panels on the north end
- **Setback:** the west verandah is about 4 m from the car park kerb, behind a row of Norfolk Island pines.

## Walls and roof

From the City's photos (2017-2018). Hex values are approximate.

- **Roof:** Colorbond Surfmist or similar, `#e4e2d5`, about 24° pitch. The gables are steeper, about 28°.
- **Walls:** pale blue weatherboard `#b3cadb`, with the same boards in the cross gables. The function room is glazed floor to ceiling under each gable.
- **Verandah:** slim charcoal posts `#3a3d40` about 4.6 m apart. The roof carries straight on over them. Concrete paving underneath.
- **Height:** walls about 3.2 m, main ridge about 7 m.
- **Signage:** none seen.

## What a driver sees

From the car park road on the west, through the pines:

1. A long pale roof sweeping down over a deep verandah.
2. A blue weatherboard gable in the middle, with big glass under it.
3. Dark posts along the edge.

From Geographe Bay Road on the north: its end, across the oval.
