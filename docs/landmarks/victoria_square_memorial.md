# Busselton War Memorial, Victoria Square

## What it is

The Busselton War Memorial is a sandstone obelisk in Victoria Square in the town centre. It was built in 1919 and unveiled on 6 January 1920. Its name tablets cover WWI (granite, about 180 names), WWII, Korea and Vietnam (bronze). A low wall just south of it holds soldiers' ashes. Victoria Square is a small park of lawn and big trees on the curve of the street, and the memorial is its only built feature.

In 2024 the RSL and the City opened a **new memorial in Rotary Park**, across the river. The old cenotaph was too cracked to move, so a new one was built there. The old obelisk is **still in Victoria Square on the current aerial**.

## Footprint

Measured from the Landgate aerial `out/refs/parks/vic_mem.jpg` (centre 115.34490,-33.65235, 0.049 m/px) and checked against the OSM memorial node (115.344884,-33.652290). About ±0.3 m.

- **Orientation:** the paved squares are square to north. The memorial faces the street on the north. The paths step a little east going south.
- **Obelisk:** **115.344886, -33.652301**, in the middle of the upper square.
- **Upper square:** about 10.2 x 9.8 m of pale paving, with short side arms (about 12.8 m across in all) and a path about 3 m wide coming in from the north footpath. Clipped dark hedges in low planters fill the four corners.
- **Lower square:** about 7.3 x 6.3 m, about 10-16 m south of the obelisk. Long planters about 3 x 8 m with low rendered walls stand either side, and a quarter-circle bed sits on its south-east.
- **Path south:** about 3 m wide, on to the south lawn, about 23 m from the obelisk.
- **Other:** a bench on the east lawn, an interpretive sign and a pole (probably a flagpole) east of the upper square.

## Walls and roof

- **Obelisk:** from the heritage record: Donnybrook sandstone and limestone, "1500 mm high and 600 mm wide on each of its four sides". The aerial shadow says the whole thing is about 3-4 m high. Drawn as:
  - a 0.6 m square shaft with a pyramid top, about 3.6 m in all
  - on a 1 m die with a grey granite tablet (`#8a8b8d`) on the north face
  - on two limestone steps
- **Colours:** sandstone `#d9c7a0`, limestone `#e6dcc4`, paving `#d8d4ca`.

## What a driver sees

From the street on the north, past the footpath: a pale obelisk on its steps at the end of a short path, framed by dark clipped hedges, with the park's big trees behind.

## Unsure

- **The obelisk's real height and profile.** The heritage record's 1.5 m may be the shaft alone.
- **Whether the old memorial stays** now that the new one is in Rotary Park. The City's Victoria Square draft master plan may change the square.
- The pole east of the square: a flagpole or a light. Not drawn.
