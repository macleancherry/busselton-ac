# Caves Road, Wilyabrup to Margaret River

**Tier:** drive-anywhere. **Route:** `routes/caves-road-margaret.json`. **Build:** `out/caves_road_wilyabrup_to_margaret_river.zip`.

Caves Road (Tourist Drive 250) south from Wilyabrup, where [Yallingup to Wilyabrup](../caves_road_yallingup_to_wilyabrup/README.md) ends, past the Gracetown and Carters Road turns, through vineyards and bush to just before Wallcliffe Road, the turn-off into Margaret River. Point to point, about 18.4 km. The finish is about 200 m short of the junction.

## Status

- **Route ready**, first build pending. All pins on Caves Road, mid-block; OSRM routes it as one road, no U-turns.
- **Heights:** not on GA's 5 m LiDAR; about 30 m global heights.
- **Budget:** the longest country route yet. If `budget.ts` fails, set `build.terrainStep` to 3, or move the split north.
- **Not included:** Wallcliffe Road into town (4.2 km to the Bussell Highway roundabout and the [Margaret River](../margaret_river/README.md) track); it would take this past the budget.
