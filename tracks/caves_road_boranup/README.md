# Caves Road, Boranup

**Tier:** drive-anywhere base (Boranup a showcase later, with 3D karri). **Route:** `routes/caves-road-boranup.json`. **Build:** `out/caves_road_boranup.zip`.

Caves Road (Tourist Drive 250) south of Margaret River, from near Mammoth and Lake caves through the Boranup karri forest to just south of where Boranup Drive comes back in. Point to point, about 16 km, sealed and winding.

## Status

- **Route:** ready, not built yet. OSRM routes the pins with no U-turns. Pins stay clear of the Boranup Drive and Allan Road junctions.
- **Heights:** not on GA's 5 m LiDAR; about 30 m global heights.
- **Boranup Drive** (the unsealed limestone scenic drive just west, about 14 km; OSM "Boranup Drive", ends near -34.099 and -34.182) isn't in this route. It's for the gravel work: a later layout or route with the force-unsealed option. OSRM doesn't route along it, so it will need its route taken from the OSM way (like the Collie circuit) or its pins checked once that option exists.
