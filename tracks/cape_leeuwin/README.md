# Cape Leeuwin

**Tier:** drive-anywhere base (showcase later, with the lighthouse). **Route:** `routes/cape-leeuwin.json`. **Build:** `out/cape_leeuwin.zip`.

Augusta to Cape Leeuwin: Blackwood Avenue through the town, then Leeuwin Road south past the water wheel turn to the lighthouse car park. Point to point, about 7.5 km.

## Status

- **Built** 2 Nov; every check passes, 0.89M triangles. The route ends in the lighthouse car park.
- **Landmarks:** the lighthouse as a Blender layer (`routes/layers/cape_leeuwin/`), the keepers' cottages and the waterwheel as specs (`routes/landmarks/cape_leeuwin_looks.json`), on a landmark patch of detailed ground off the road.
- **Heights:** not on GA's 5 m LiDAR; about 30 m global heights.
- **In game:** not driven yet; one AI run in retest 6.
