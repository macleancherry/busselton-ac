# Caves Road, Dunsborough to Yallingup

**Tier:** drive-anywhere (Yallingup itself a showcase area later). **Route:** `routes/caves-road.json`.

Caves Road (Tourist Drive 250) from just west of the Dunsborough roundabout, along the coast through peppermint and marri, climbing to Yallingup and on south past the Ngilgi Cave turn. Point to point, about 11 km. The first South West track.

## Status

- **Route:** ready.
- **Heights:** GA's 5 m LiDAR covers only the Dunsborough end. The rest falls back to about 30 m global heights, which smooth the hills. Sharper heights would come from the capes LiDAR on GA's ELVIS portal (free, links emailed).
