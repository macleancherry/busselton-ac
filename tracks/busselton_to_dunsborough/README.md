# Busselton to Dunsborough

**Tier:** drive-anywhere. **Route:** `routes/busselton-dunsborough.json`. **Build:** `out/busselton_to_dunsborough.zip`.

Caves Road (Tourist Drive 250) west from the end of the Busselton dual carriageway, along the flat coastal strip behind Geographe Bay, through the Dunsborough roundabouts to just past the Cape Naturaliste Road roundabout. Point to point, about 14 km. It ends where [Caves Road, Dunsborough to Yallingup](../caves_road_yallingup/README.md) starts.
